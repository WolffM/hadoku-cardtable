# v0.68.0 — Undo

Undo arrives beside Restart turn, Bomberman plays through the room, and a restart keeps what you were shown.

## New

- **Undo takes back your last action: a land, a cast or an activation.** It sits beside Restart turn and asks your opponent first; the AI always allows. It reaches back to when your opponent last had priority, and a draw, a reveal or anything random ends it. (@bicyclops)
- **Bomberman plays through the room.** A Bomberman room's match runs on Cardtable itself, so everyone in the room is in the same game, and the room shows who won.
- A Bomberman room plays as many matches as you like: the leader starts the next one in the same room.

## Changed

- Forge's own "Undo (N)" no longer appears beside Resolve; a tapped land still untaps when you click it.
- In Bomberman, a player who drops keeps their seat for two minutes to come back.
- In Bomberman, whoever joins after every seat is taken watches, and everyone keeps the seat the room shows them after a reconnect.

## Fixed

- Restarting a turn no longer forgets cards you were shown.
- A match no longer stops with an engine error when you answer a prompt the moment it appears.
- When the rules engine fails on your move, the game ends and tells both players instead of freezing.
- A card in your hand no longer lights up for a moment on a step the game passes for you.
- An AI seat in a pod never wears the same character as a drafter.
- In Bomberman, only the leader's game can end a round.
- A Bomberman room opened after a round ended shows who won.

## Fixes

- Games now park and resume from one saved checkpoint, the same path every game will use.
- The engine handles each player's moves in that player's own order, so a move can't race the update it answers.
- Bomberman's rooms, results and rematches run on the same relay any future game will use.
- A failing engine test now keeps its full evidence, so intermittent failures can be traced.
- Card art for the cards the new undo and restart scenarios use.
