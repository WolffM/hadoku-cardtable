# v0.71.0 — Open doors and who's coming

A room link now lets anyone in, and the calendar shows who signed up.

## New

- **See who signed up for an event.** The calendar's sign-up count is a row of faces you can press to see everyone, including who is already in the room. The same list opens from beside the event room's name.

## Changed

- A room link opens for anyone: a first-time visitor gets an account on the spot, named Player and a number.
- An event's sign-up count counts everyone who answered its poll or is in its room, once each.
- The calendar shows each event's details one per line, so nothing wraps.
- Handing the room to someone else asks you to confirm first.
- A tournament room's card shows how many players are in it, not "4/8".
- The UI scale slider is in the Game settings now; the Display tab is gone.
- A draft room has no "Fewer seats" button; the leader closes a seat with its own Close.

## Fixed

- Dragging across a calendar or poll entry selects its text instead of turning the page.
- The chat's list of who is here shows each person once, even across tabs.
- An event made from a Rally sign-up form names the form's creator as its organizer, who can move or cancel it.
- A draft event in Discord and a draft room's link preview say what the pod drafts, not "Cube Draft".
- A character whose art was redrawn shows the new art everywhere, link previews included.

## Fixes

- Groundwork for more games on the same table: shared room, page and stats parts.
- Groundwork for guest accounts.
- Stricter code checks and test updates.
- Dependency updates.
