# v0.78.0 — Pick your printing

Choose the art each of your cards is drawn with, leave a room with one press, and a tidier Gameplay settings tab.

## New

- **Pick your card art.** The Library's new Cards tab finds any card and lines up its printings, oldest first, each with its set and year. Press one and that's how your copy is drawn in every match; Reset to default puts it back.
- "Always use oldest art" is a switch in Settings: every card you haven't picked by hand is drawn as its oldest printing. (@coffeetraveler)
- Rooms have a Leave button at the start of their top row, which takes you back to the front page.
- Friends can open a Crossover Cards room, Cozmiverse's card game for 2 to 4 players.

## Changed

- Clicking Card Table in the top bar from a room now leaves the room too.
- Settings: Board layout and Game speed each sit on one line, at the top of Gameplay.

## Fixes

- Groundwork for cube and set stats: drafts and matches now record which cube or set they used.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
- Dependency updates.
- Planning notes.
