# v0.66.1 — Nothing lost

Clicks, land taps and hits that went missing on a busy server or a dropped connection now arrive, and the server no longer stalls.

## Fixed

- A click made while your connection was dropping is no longer lost: it is sent once you are back at the table.
- Drawing a box to tap several lands no longer stops partway on a busy server.
- Giving up a Mox Opal's colour choice now untaps it every time.
- Damage from a resolving spell or ability now always flies to the player's face, even when the server is busy.
- An attacking creature is always drawn charging at you: your life no longer drops in combat with nobody hitting you.
- The server no longer freezes for a second or two while it refreshes its card list, or while an account is deleted.

## Fixes

- More groundwork for running other games on the same tables and rooms.
- Card art for more of the cards scenarios and decks place.
- Steadier automated tests.
- Planning notes, and the stronger computer opponent stays off.
- Dependency updates.
