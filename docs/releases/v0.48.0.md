# v0.48.0 — Magic on your phone

Magic is playable on a phone held upright, and linking your Discord gives you a role and your Card Table name there.

## New

- **Magic is playable on a phone held upright.** You and your opponent sit side by side at the top, the board is four lanes that scroll sideways, your hand is a carousel above an action bar on the bottom edge (Chat, Menu, zoom and the next step), and pinch or double-tap zooms in.
- **On a phone, a decision docks above the action bar, only as tall as its question.** A long search opens full screen, a question about your hand (a mulligan's cards to put back) sits above your hand, and "–" shrinks a decision to a pill you can tap to bring back.
- **On a phone, a tap on a hand card or a stack item shows it large, with what you can do with it.** A tap on a permanent taps it, attacks or targets with it, and a long press reads it. Drag a hand card up to play it, drag a spell waiting for its target onto the target, drag attackers onto a player or a planeswalker and a blocker onto an attacker.
- On a phone, creatures, artifacts, enchantments and planeswalkers sit in front and lands behind, and tokens stack by name with their count.
- On a phone, your floating mana shows on your seat at the top, and a tap on a symbol spends it.
- **Linking your Discord gives you a Linked role in the Card Table Discord.** Your nickname there becomes your Card Table name, so people can tell who you are at the table. Link it from the settings gear.
