# v0.79.0 — Sort and star your cubes

Find the cubes and sets people actually play, and Crossover Cards sets its own player count.

## New

- **Cubes and sets sort by how much they are played, and you can star your favourites.** The Library's Cubes tab and a draft room's Cube and Set tabs have a search box and a sort: newest, most drafted, most played or name. A starred cube or set stays first in every sort.

## Changed

- Crossover Cards sets its own player count, down to one player against its own bots; new rooms open again once its next update is out.
