# v0.34.0 — Card Table has a Discord

Card Table now has its own Discord server, and these notes are its first automatic post. Big turns
also got a lot smoother.

## New

- **A Discord button in the top bar**, next to the theme picker. It opens the invite, and joining
  makes you a Player. Patch notes land in #announcements, tournaments in #events, and
  #looking-for-game is for finding a table.
- **Patch notes post themselves.** Every release announces what changed here, the moment the main
  table updates.

## Fixed

- **A big stack no longer slows the game to a crawl.** Every card waiting on the stack used to make
  the browser redraw its shadow on every frame. A Pyroclasm that put twenty-odd triggers on the
  stack dropped the game to about 7 frames a second. The cards still float and drift, and their
  shadow now holds still.
- **Big fights stay smooth.** When several creatures die or crash together, the dust, shards and
  rocks are now shared out among them instead of each throwing its full share. A four-creature
  pile-up looks about as busy as two, instead of putting over two hundred moving pieces on screen.
- **Flying creatures cost far less to draw**, and their bob stays steady and in step, Firefox
  included. The counters and badges on them stay right on the card.
- **Shroud's mist no longer redraws the card it covers on every frame.** The fog drifts across the
  card as it always did.
- **The board no longer makes the whole page work out its styles again on every change.** In a
  big attack, that work is now about half what it was.
- **Rematch in a board-state room** replays the scripted board, instead of turning the game into a
  best-of-three and stopping at a sideboard step.
