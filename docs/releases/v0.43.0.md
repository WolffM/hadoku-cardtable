# v0.43.0 — Big drafts, a chess clock, and harder hits

Draft rooms past 8 players, a chess clock and pick timer for tournaments, and creatures that wind up and punch.

## New

- **Draft rooms are no longer capped at 8 players.** Once everyone is ready, the room's owner presses Split & randomize, everyone sees their pod and the seats lock, then Start draft deals each pod its own draft. Everyone then plays one tournament of up to 3 rounds with a single standings.
- **A chess clock for matches.** Each player has 20 minutes for a whole match, shown under their life total, running only while the game waits on them; run out and you lose the match. It's on by default in tournaments and drafts, and any room can switch it in Match rules.
- **Drafts can have a pick timer.** The room leader turns it on in the draft lobby and picks a speed; a burning rope shows for the last third, and the draft bot picks for you if time runs out.
- **Create event makes a Tournament or a Form.** A tournament takes all of its room's settings; a form is a Rally sign-up form, made on Rally or pasted in by link.
- **The front page's Upcoming card is now Calendar and Polls.** Calendar lists upcoming events with View and Sign up, Polls lists open sign-up forms; flip between them with the arrows, the dots or a swipe.
- The room leader can reset a tournament game from scratch, keeping the match score.
- The room leader can disqualify a player: their match is a loss, their next opponent gets a bye.

## Changed

- **Creatures wind up before they hit a player.** Each draws back, then slams into the avatar; the bigger the creature, the deeper the wind-up, the harder the hit and the slower it lumbers home. A big hit shakes and cracks the table, and several attackers hit one after another, weakest first.
- **Stack cards bump and spread instead of shrinking.** They pile when the board is full and never cover End Turn. Arrows start near a card's top middle and curve so their heads point into their target, and the corner brackets show only while you choose a target.
- **Settings live in one place, in tabs.** The gear at the top right of every screen holds Game, Sound and Display, each in its own tab, and reopens on the one you last used. Your sound settings and music track follow you between Card Table, watchparty and your devices.
- Leaving a tournament room mid-round counts as dropping out, and your opponent wins at once.
- A tournament plays at most three Swiss rounds, and standings never end in a shared place.
- A tournament hub draws each table as one slim line, with its actions behind one ⋯ button.
- The opponent's seat bar mirrors yours: clock, life and mark line up across the centre.
- Rooms that aren't tournaments close after 8 hours idle, never during a game.
- A game left running for 8 hours ends in a draw, and both players see why.
- A room you can enter says Join, whether or not you're already in it.
- The front page loads in one step: placeholders first, then everything fades in together.
- Room cards are all one height; a room's playing or drafting word sits beside its buttons.
- The front page's wildcard card can be flipped through again.
- Organizers move or cancel their own events right in the calendar list.
- The calendar always shows what an event's room is set to now.
- Switching eras in a constructed room no longer makes the deck grid jump or pop.
- A creature's landing sound gets heavier as its landing does, and artifact and colourless creatures can have their own voice.
- Your match-music review is kept with your account, and a new one replaces your last.
