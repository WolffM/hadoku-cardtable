# v0.38.0 — One picker for one of each, and room for 50 games

"One of each" choices ask once, and games move to their own machines with room for fifty at a time.

## Changed

- **Games run on their own machines.** Up to 50 games can be played at once, up from 10. If a
  machine fails, its games carry on from the start of the turn on another.
- **"One of each" choices are one picker.** Shard Convergence and every "search for a this and a
  that", Cataclysm, Global Ruin, Celestial Judgment, Slaughter the Strong, Niv-Mizzet Reborn,
  Michelangelo's Technique and Battle for Bretagard ask once, and a card with no type left to fill
  is greyed out.
- A permanent put onto the battlefield straight from a library (Natural Order, Collected Company)
  now lands with its weight too.
- New room card symbols: a draft room shows a card with pass arrows and a pod passing packs round
  the table; a constructed room shows a deck and two decks squared up. Classic's crossed swords
  now look like swords.

## Fixes

- Card art entries for new test scenarios.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Hosting test results and planning notes.
- Dependency updates.
