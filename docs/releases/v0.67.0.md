# v0.67.0 — Bomberman joins the table

Bomberman rooms open beside Magic, a room keeps one chat and can be reset, and every creature lands with its entry.

## New

- **Bomberman is here.** Pick Bomberman when you make a room, and play it right alongside your Magic rooms. A Bomberman room survives a server restart like any other.
- A room's leader can Reset it, beside Close: every table starts over and everyone stays.
- With the chat hidden, new messages float over the table for a few seconds.

## Changed

- A room keeps one chat, from the lobby through every game at every table.
- Your character is your account's, the same in every room and every hadoku app.
- Making an account is just a name: no passkey prompt, in any browser.
- Right after you sign up, a "Save a passkey?" card by the settings gear offers to save one.

## Fixed

- A creature put onto the battlefield by Show and Tell, Sneak Attack or cascade now lands with its entry, as does one the AI casts.
- After a library search, Oracle of Mul Daya shows your top card to you again.
- A Gauntlet whose opponent left the room frees the table again.
- Your opponent's targeting arrow starts from the card they aim with, not its neighbour.
- The year slider no longer greys out 1995, 2005 and 2015.
- The Card Table title takes you back to the rooms from inside a room again.

## Fixes

- More groundwork for running other games on the same tables and rooms.
- Slow prompts now report their timing, so a stall can be traced.
- The machines that host games restart more cleanly.
- Card art for more of the cards scenarios and decks place.
- Steadier automated tests and nightly checks.
- Planning notes.
- Dependency updates.
