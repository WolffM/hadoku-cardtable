# v0.47.0 — Stats, tournaments with a podium, and a smoother table

A Stats page, tournaments with a real bracket and a podium, games you can pick back up, and big turns that stutter far less.

## New

- **Stats is a page of its own.** The trophy opens a Leaderboard: rankings with each rating's 30-day move, the decks and colours that win most, and every rated match. Your profile has your rank, a chart of your last 30 matches, unfinished games, recent matches, how you do against each player, and your decks and colours. Search any player, or click a name.
- **A podium when a tournament ends.** The top three stand on medal blocks with their characters, the winner crowned, under confetti.
- **Pick up a game you walked away from.** When both players leave, the game is saved after five minutes. Press Resume under Unfinished games on your profile and it restarts at the beginning of the turn it was left on, and counts like any other.
- **Drag your opponent's spells and abilities on the stack, not just your own.** If you both grab one, it's a tug of war, and whoever lets go last places it. (@kawakimi)

## Changed

- **The tournament bracket is a Swiss bracket.** Matches sit on their players' record row, lines step winners up and losers down, and a Final column lists everyone by place. Press a player to light their path. Match cards show both faces and games won, and a live match still takes you into it.
- **Big turns stutter far less.** Cards redraw only when they change, the stack isn't re-measured after every update, and trails, glows, stack slides and exile burns cost the browser far less. Wipes, Worldfire and long stacks are smoother on both screens.
- **An animation repeated in the same turn plays faster each time,** up to 2.5×, back to normal next turn. A spell with many hits, like Blasphemous Act, counts once.
- **The Gauntlet's last pick glides the six surviving decks into the choose screen** on both screens at once, and you can choose one while it moves.
- **A Classic room's year is a slider:** drag it, tap it, or nudge it with ‹ › or the arrow keys, with longer marks every five and ten years. Free pick's browse uses it too, and non-leaders read the format plainly, e.g. "Standard 2015".
- **Every room seats you as you join while a seat is open,** with a Spectators row: click a seat to sit or move, or Spectators to watch. Stand is gone, and switching a room's mode keeps everyone where they were.
- Drafting on a phone: the fan is a swipeable carousel, and the grid is two columns of big cards.
- The draft's grid and fan views share one page.
- Standings show rank, player, record and points; Tie-breakers opens the tie-breaker columns. Watchers aren't listed under a tournament's matches any more.
- A room's rules sit behind a Room settings button beside Close.
- A profile's Decks table filters with pills and shows when each deck was last played.
- The front page opens faster, and keeps its card data after an update.
- Sounds arrive in one download and stay in your browser.
- Long matches stay quick to the end, and reconnecting loads the board as it stands, not every move.

## Fixed

- A tournament under way locks its format, year, mode and settings instead of answering a press with an error.
- A Gauntlet room no longer shows a "Try again" error while you ban and pick.
- Ability chips no longer cover a card you or your opponent can click.
- A triggered ability's card shows on the stack even when your opponent passes at once.
- A target choice no longer opens on top of the cards it asks you to pick from.
- A card you scry no longer flashes on your library before the Top/Bottom choice.
- The table loads only the music track a match plays: about 47 MB less, and phones stay responsive.
- The front page's Discord chat and polls appear at once, and pause while the tab is hidden.
- A deck's list in a room no longer scrolls the room sideways, and pop-ups open over the whole page.
- The Gauntlet's deck pool no longer cuts off names, and its last pick plays its sound.
- A deck sliding under a resting mouse no longer pops its decklist.
- The draft's fan/grid switch is no longer cut off on a laptop, and a phone's pack shows its cards without resizing.

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships, and fewer tests that fail only on a busy machine.
- Quicker server restarts, WebSocket-first connections, and fewer repeated reads.
- Match streams and recordings stay in order as a game grows.
- Better usage measurements and a daily digest to the team's Discord.
- Admin test rooms for tournaments, card art for test decks, a performance bench, and dependency updates.
