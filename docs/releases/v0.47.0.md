# v0.47.0 — Stats, tournaments with a podium, and a smoother table

A Stats page of its own, tournaments with a real bracket and a podium, games you can pick back up, and big turns that stutter far less.

## New

- **Stats is a page of its own now.** The trophy opens a Leaderboard: everyone's rankings with how far each rating moved in the last 30 days (sort them by rating, win rate, wins or games), the decks and colours that win most, and every rated match, newest first. Your profile has your rank and rating, a chart of your last 30 matches, your unfinished games, your recent matches, how you do against each player, and your decks and colours. Find any player from the box at the top, or click a name to open their profile.
- **A podium when a tournament ends.** First, second and third stand on their medal blocks with their characters, the winner crowned, under falling confetti.
- **Pick up a game you walked away from.** When both players leave a game, it waits five minutes for them and is then saved for good. Open Stats, find it under Unfinished games on your profile and press Resume: it starts again in a new room at the beginning of the turn it was left on, and a game finished that way counts like any other.
- **You can drag your opponent's spells and abilities on the stack, not just your own.** If you both grab the same one, it's a tug of war: it sits between your two hands, and whoever lets go last decides where it lands.

## Changed

- **The tournament bracket is a Swiss bracket.** Each match sits on the row of its players' record, lines step winners up and losers down, and a Final column lists everyone by place. Press a player to light their path through the rounds. Match cards show both players, their faces and games won, and never squeeze; a live match still takes you into its game.
- **Big turns stutter far less.** A card redraws only when something about it changes, the table no longer re-measures every spell on the stack after each update, and fireball trails, power and toughness glows, spells sliding on the stack and cards burning away into exile look the same but cost the browser far less. Wipes, Worldfire, long stacks and token-heavy turns are smoother on both screens.
- **An animation that plays again in the same turn plays faster each time,** up to two and a half times as fast, and the next turn starts at normal speed again. A Thopter Foundry loop speeds up each time the Sword of the Meek comes back; a spell that draws many hits, like Blasphemous Act, counts once.
- **The Gauntlet's last pick glides the decks into the choose screen.** The six surviving decks fly from the board into their seats and the banned ones fall away, on both players' screens at once. A deck can be chosen while it is still moving.
- **A Classic room's year is a slider,** not a dropdown: a ruler of years with longer marks every five and ten. Drag it, tap anywhere on it, or nudge it a year with ‹ and › or the arrow keys; the deck grid changes once you let go. Free pick's year browse is the same slider, with "All" at its left end, and players who aren't leading read the room's format plainly, e.g. "Standard 2015".
- **Every room seats you as you join while a seat is open,** so the first people in are the players, and nobody who joins later sits ahead of someone already waiting. Rooms show a Spectators row: click an open seat to sit or move, or the open slot in Spectators to watch, and the Stand button is gone everywhere. Switching a room between Constructed and Draft, or turning Tournament on or off, keeps its players playing and its spectators watching.
- Drafting on a phone: the fan is a carousel you swipe, one card in the middle with its neighbours peeking in, and the grid shows two columns of big cards across the whole width.
- The draft's grid view uses the same page as the fan: switching changes only how the pack's cards are laid out.
- Standings show rank, player, record and points; Tie-breakers opens the tie-breaker columns, with the one that separated players level on points marked. Nobody watching is listed under a tournament's matches any more: the chat shows who is in the room.
- A room's rules (hand smoothing, free pick, the chess clock) are behind a Room settings button beside Close.
- The Decks table on a profile filters with pills, and says when each deck was last played.
- The front page opens faster: it no longer waits for the whole game to download, and after an update your browser keeps the card data it already has.
- Game sounds arrive in one download instead of two hundred, and sounds you've already heard stay in your browser.
- Long matches stay as quick to respond at the end as at the start, and coming back to one is quick even on a phone: a reconnect loads the board as it stands and this turn's moves, not every move of the game.

## Fixed

- A tournament under way no longer offers its format, year, mode, Tournament switch or room settings to change; they are read-only from the deal on, instead of answering a press with an error.
- A Gauntlet room no longer shows "a Gauntlet game starts once both decks are chosen" with Try again while you ban and pick.
- Ability chips on the stack no longer cover a card you or your opponent can click; a long chain of triggers, like Cephalid Breakfast's, piles up in open table instead.
- Opening the table no longer loads every music track, only the one a match plays: about 47 MB less to download and far less memory, so phones stay responsive.
- The front page's Discord chat and event polls appear at once, a Rally outage no longer stalls the polls card, and the calendar and polls stop checking for news while the tab is hidden.
- A deck's list opened in a room no longer makes the room scroll sideways, and it sits right beside its tile. Match rules, settings, stats and the create-room form open over the whole page.
- A card you scry no longer flashes on top of your library before the Top/Bottom choice opens.
- A triggered ability's card now shows on the stack even when your opponent passes on it right away.
- A target choice no longer opens on top of the cards it asks you to pick from.
- The Gauntlet's deck pool no longer cuts off deck names or pilots, on a phone or on desktop.
- The Gauntlet's last pick plays its pick sound.
- A deck sliding under a resting mouse no longer pops open its decklist.
- On a laptop-sized window, the draft's fan/grid switch is no longer cut off.
- On a phone, the draft's pack shows its cards in both views, and the page no longer keeps resizing itself.

## Fixes

- Faster, steadier checks before an update ships, and far fewer tests that fail only on a busy machine.
- The server starts listening before it sets up its test rooms, so a restart is quicker.
- Connections open over WebSocket first, and the server reads the deck library and sound list once instead of on every request.
- A match's live stream and its recording stay open and in order as a game grows.
- Usage measurements now record slow scrolls correctly, and a daily digest of how the table is used is posted to the team's Discord.
- The server runs on the fleet's shared Java install.
- Admin-only test rooms for a finished and an in-progress tournament.
- Card art entries for new test decks, a performance bench, planning notes and dependency updates.
