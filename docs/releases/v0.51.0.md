# v0.51.0 — Your avatar, everywhere

Your avatar picture and cosmetics now show on every face and name at the table, and the front page plays a lively recent game when none is live.

## New

- **Your avatar is the same everywhere at the table.** The picture you chose in your avatar settings (Discord, Steam or an upload) shows on your seat, your board portrait, the coin toss, the result screen, the draft seats, the bracket, standings and podium.
- Overlays, name effects, gradients and fonts you wear show on every face and name too.

## Changed

- When no game is live, the front page shows a random recent game instead of always the last one, favouring games with lots happening and little waiting.
- Release posts credit reporters inline in their bullet, with no separate thank-you line, and a corrected release note updates its post.

## Fixed

- The front page's Discord chat stays current: it updates every hour, even on a page left open.
- Watching a game or a replay, the last player's cursor no longer lingers under their name after the turn passes.

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Dependency updates.
