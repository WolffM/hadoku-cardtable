# v0.74.0 — No top bar at the table

The draft, the deck builder, the sideboard and the game lose the top bar, the draft's fan takes drags, and Cogwork Librarian and pasted cube lists arrive.

## New

- **Drag a card out of the pack to take it.** Let it go over your picks to take it, or over the sideboard to take it straight into your sideboard; anywhere else and it goes back. Drag the fan sideways to scroll through the pack.
- An (i) button by the pack lists the draft's controls.
- **Cogwork Librarian works in a draft.** It is drafted face up for the whole table. While you hold it, press its name by the pack, then two cards, and you take both; the Librarian goes into that pack for the next player. (@thyeggman)
- **A cube from a card list.** The draft room's Import box takes a pasted list as well as a Cube Cobra link: one card a line, or a Cube Cobra export. Name it and it is saved to your Inventory.
- Admins have a testing room, "Testing: Draft pod — one pick left", whose AI opponent gives up every game on its second turn, to walk a draft's end, a whole series and the next round in minutes.

## Changed

- **No top bar in the draft, the deck builder, the sideboard or the game.** Home, the theme picker, settings, Discord and GitHub are in a row at the top of the chat, and the table takes the freed space.
- The fan/grid switch moved to the room's bar, beside Close and the new (i) button.
- On your own table's page the way on is one plain button under the two players (Enter game, Return to game or Sideboard), with concede and drop beside it. Enter game after sideboarding is that same button.
- When a match is over, Back to room is the lit button on the result.
- An imported cube becomes the room's cube at once, and cards Forge can't play are named right away.
- A link to any of a cube's Cube Cobra pages imports it, not only its overview and list pages.

## Fixed

- An ability that removes all counters from its own card (Sage of Hours, Jar of Eyeballs) no longer asks "Remove all counters?" first.
- A Forage cost asks whether to sacrifice a Food when you could exile three cards instead; it was always the Food.
- A pile of ability chips on the stack no longer covers a player's face, so a player can always be targeted.
- Inventory names who imported a cube instead of showing an id, and offers Delete on your own imports.
- A testing room's stand-in keeps its name after a restart.

## Fixes

- Every Bloomburrow card is now cast against the real rules engine with each of its choices taken and refused, before an update ships.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
