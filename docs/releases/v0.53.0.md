# v0.53.0 — Drag to your colour

Press a dual land and drag to its colour, restarted and moved games keep far more of their state, and updates no longer keep a match away for extra seconds.

## New

- **Press a dual land and drag to its colour.** The mana ring opens under your press: slide to a colour and let go to tap for it, or let go anywhere else to tap nothing. A plain click works as before.

## Changed

- A land that opens the mana ring can no longer be dragged to rearrange it on the battlefield: a drag on it now picks from its ring.

## Fixed

- A game restarted from its turn's start, or moved to another server mid-game, keeps its emblems, boons, cards on an adventure, effects that last until a later turn, creatures exiled "until this leaves the battlefield", and hexproof from a permanent; a turn whose effects can't yet be put back exactly can no longer be restarted.
- While paying a cost, a colour on the mana ring that pays nothing you owe is greyed out, and a filter land's pair is asked for only once.
- On a phone, the mana ring opens above your finger, and a swipe across a lane of dual lands scrolls it again; a slide toward a colour still picks it.
- A server restart, for an update or the nightly maintenance, no longer sometimes keeps your match away for about ten extra seconds.
- The draft opens faster.
- In a replay, a card a player plays no longer leaves a card back standing beside it after it lands.

## Fixes

- cardtable's data is backed up every night, off the machine, with an alarm if a backup goes stale.
- Updates start faster: the Java bridge is compiled in the build.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Card art for new test scenarios and decks.
- Dependency updates.
