# v0.42.0 — Replays, the stack, and a smoother table

Replays work again with a close-up of the active player's corner, the stack makes room for every card, and the table moves more smoothly.

## New

- **Replays show the active player's cursor and drags as they happened.** When they park the cursor elsewhere on the table, the front page's card glides over to follow it, and back once they return. This covers games played from this release on.

## Changed

- **The front page's game card is a square close-up of the active player's corner.** It shows their avatar, life, graveyard and the near half of their hand and battlefield, and turns around at every turn.
- **Cards on the stack make room for each other.** A spell let go on top of another slides to the free spot beside it, and a deep stack shrinks to fit. Arrows between stack cards are drawn over them.
- A permanent let go anywhere off your hand is played, into its row's next free space.
- When the other player counters or removes something, you can act as its animation finishes; your own spells never make you wait.
- In a draft, each player holds the pack they're picking from in front of their portrait.
- A loading draft shows its own outline: face-down cards, the pod's seats and the pick counters.

## Fixed

- **Replays work again, on the front page and at a replay's own link.** Seeking, scrubbing, every speed and the loop no longer stop on "stopped after 3 resyncs"; both hands show face up with their art.
- The front page's replay card appears once its board is ready, never on "Connecting…".
- Playable cards no longer flash dark between phases.
- A cast creature lands once, without a highlight or a jerk on the way.
- In a board wipe each creature dies once, after its own fireball hits it.
- An ability's chip appears beside its card with its art, long enough to read.
- An opponent's spell no longer stays face down on the stack.
- A click on a permanent is no longer swallowed by an ability leaving the stack.
- Battalion cards like Frontline Medic no longer glow orange during attacks.
- Both players Ready goes straight to the board, with no "Connecting…" screen.
- A match link opened as the room carries you in no longer sticks on "Reconnecting…".
- A tournament's face-off uses the table page's whole width.
- A match card's phase is a plain label, not a button-like pill.
- A game conceded before the coin toss no longer shows Play/Draw under the result.
- A draft no longer shrinks and slides its cards as it finishes loading.

## Fixes
