# v0.52.0 — A ring of mana, and a roomier phone

Lands pick their colour from a ring at your cursor, the phone game gets a new top band and bottom bar, you can restart a turn, and updates now pause a game for a few seconds instead of half a minute.

## New

- **Choosing a colour from a land opens a ring of mana symbols right where you clicked.** Duals, pain lands, City of Brass, filter lands and every other multi-colour source show all their results at once, with life, damage and other costs marked on each, and a land's other abilities sit on the ring as chips. One click (or a W/U/B/R/G/C key) taps it; on a phone the ring opens just above your finger.
- **Restart turn.** On your own turn, ask to play it again from its start, and your opponent allows or declines; the AI always allows. (@the.scrubdaddy)
- Deleting your hadoku account now erases your cardtable data too: your saved decks and parked games are deleted, and the rooms, tournaments and events you shared with others no longer name you.
- Past tournaments and replays show each player's avatar, look and the name they go by now.

## Changed

- **On a phone held upright, the game has a new top band and one bottom bar.** Both players sit in the top band with their life, name, hand count and graveyard, exile and library buttons, with poison, energy, experience, the monarch or the initiative and floating mana under each. The bar holds the button for whatever you're deciding, or who you're waiting for; Chat, Menu and zoom are behind ⋯. (@rhomancer)
- On a phone, your hand rests as a strip of card tops and rises when you tap it, giving the board about 130px more room; empty rows fold away, sparse rows draw bigger and crowded rows wrap; and after a pinch-zoom you can drag the board up and down. (@rhomancer)
- An update mid-game now says "Updating…" instead of "Reconnecting…", and the game is back in a few seconds instead of about thirty.
- The "Mana floating" warning opens right where you clicked to pass; on a phone, just above your finger. (@the.scrubdaddy)
- The "Proceed to …?" button glows when there is nothing left to do this step.
- A new room starts with a name like "Alice's Tavern", ready to keep or change.
- A member the operator has verified now has everything a friend has, including the match-music review.
- The front page's replay camera follows a cursor that leaves its corner after half a second and eases into each move.
- The front page's patch notes show as many of the newest notes as fit, instead of three.
- Rally's Pack preview takes the arrow keys at once, shows just the cube or set's name, and deals the pack face down while it loads.
- Event organizers, form attachers and past tournament players show the name they go by now; a replay rebuilt after its first week calls the players "Player 1" and "Player 2" in the game's own text.

## Fixed

- A game restarted from its turn's start, or moved to another server mid-game, keeps the monarch, the initiative, day or night, speed, rad counters, every stolen creature with the right player, and "at the beginning of your next upkeep" effects.
- The front page's replay picks each card up from the player's hand and lands it in its column, with no copy left behind.
- On a phone, a swipe on the board always scrolls it, in combat too, and swiping while choosing a target no longer cancels the spell. (@rhomancer)
- On a phone, tapping a graveyard or exile count opens that pile. (@rhomancer)
- Clicking quickly while choosing cards (Brainstorm's "put two back") never plays a card once the choice is done.
- Clicking a spell you can't pay for always says "I don't have enough mana for that."
- Player names on the board's seat rail, the Gauntlet result, the face-off and the spectator list show their name effects.
- The stats board and profile show each player's own avatar, not a letter.
- A chain of stack items no longer rushes when the screen was slow to draw the last one.

## Fixes

- Faster recovery after an update, and the server's data kept outside its code so updates build alongside the running game.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Card art for new test scenarios and decks.
- Dependency updates.
