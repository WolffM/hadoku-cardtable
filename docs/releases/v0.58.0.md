# v0.58.0 — Bots in the Swiss

Draft pods with bots now play a full Swiss, every room keeps its settings in one place, and a game knows which tab you're playing it in.

## New

- **Bots in a draft pod are real Swiss players.** Every bot you seat plays the deck it drafted and is paired, ranked and given byes like anyone else. Two bots paired together play their match out, and the bracket shows its score.
- **One live tab per game.** Opening a game you're already playing in another tab or on another device asks whether to play here instead. Yes moves the game to this tab, and the other tab gets a Play here button to take it back. No takes you to the front page. Reloading the page you're playing on never asks.

## Changed

- The chess clock is 20 minutes per player for each game, and running out of time loses that game, not the whole match.
- A draft room's pick timer, chess clock and bots live in Room settings by the Close button, like every other room's rules, and draft rooms can now switch hand smoothing.
- Tournament bracket: your own live match pulses instead of showing a Return to game button, and the whole card takes you back in.
- Tournament bracket: each live match shows its game number, the record label over each group is gone, and a match's ⋯ menu opens in a pop-up.
- Attacking a player with no planeswalker out draws no arrow: there was nothing to pick.
- The front page only shows games between two people.
- Your avatar comes from your account, and picking a new one mid-match redraws it on both boards.
- Cardtable has its own tab icon: a sly fox peering over its hand of cards.

## Fixed

- A card clicked just as the game passes your priority is played once you get priority back, instead of being turned away.
- A click made just as the game moves to a new decision is no longer turned away, and never lands on a decision you didn't see.
- Cancel on the "Yielding until end of turn" bar always stops the yield.
- OK and End Turn come back at once after a refused click, instead of staying grey for ten seconds.
- A card you targeted no longer stays marked after its ability is on the stack.
- A targeting arrow no longer lingers after its spell or ability resolves.
- Enter or Space on a button right after dragging a card works the first time.
- Leaving between games of a best-of-three no longer loses your seat or your deck.
- A tournament round that starts while you pick a deck no longer stops on "load a deck first".
- A game lost on the clock says "Out of Time", not "conceded".
- A slow-connecting match no longer sticks on "Connected, not seated."
- A game tab left open across an update reloads itself and carries on.
- Opening a room from an invite link no longer resets the page as you join.
- A draft room's chat runs the full height of the window, like every other room's.
- A small set-cube never repeats a pack, and a pack never holds the same card twice.
- A set-cube option's sample pack in Rally shows set-cube packs.

## Fixes

- Groundwork for more games on the same platform: shared packages for the screen chrome, sound, the server's startup and the wire, MIT licensed, with a check that keeps Magic code out of them.
- Saved rooms move to a new format and storage, read back without loss.
- Steadier, faster checks before an update ships: the automated test player covers more and exits cleanly, test timing fixes, stricter landing checks, and test runs no longer take each other's machines.
- Better logs for clicks the game drops and the AI's plays, and a sturdier daily telemetry report.
- Card art rows for new test scenarios, and dependency updates.
