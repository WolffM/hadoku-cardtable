# v0.72.0 — Who the table waits on

A tournament table between games says what it is waiting for, and an organizer can tidy their sign-up list.

## New

- An event's organizer can remove someone from its sign-up list, which also takes them out of the room.

## Changed

- A tournament table between games says what it is waiting for and on whom, shows who is away and for how long, lists the games played, and counts the round's finished matches.
- A lone blocker on a menace attacker has a cracked, faded block arrow that shakes now and then, on both screens; a second blocker turns both arrows solid. This replaces the broken-shield mark.
- Attacking and blocking creatures no longer wear the crossed-swords and shield chips; their outlines, tilt and arrows show the same thing.

## Fixed

- A floating cosmetic stays on its owner's face when you scroll a list of players.
- After a restart, a draft event in Discord names its set in full (Bloomburrow) straight away, not its code (BLB).
- The mistake speech bubble sits level with your face, and its tail meets the bubble cleanly.

## Fixes

- The page downloads less: it shares hadoku.me's one copy of React.
- Groundwork for guest accounts and for more games on the same table.
- Steadier checks before an update ships, and a shorter README.
- Dependency updates.
