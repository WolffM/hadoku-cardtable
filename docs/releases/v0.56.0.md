# v0.56.0 — Pick your bots

A draft room's leader now picks what the bots draft with, the deck editor zooms and pans, and a phone hand stays in one smooth row.

## New

- **A draft room's leader picks what the bots draft with.** The draft model, the cube's pick order, or a split pod with half of each.
- The deck editor and the sideboard step zoom and pan: pinch or ctrl+scroll to zoom, drag to move around, and tap the zoom percentage to go back to 100%.

## Changed

- On a phone, deck cards stay big enough to read; swipe to see the rest.

## Fixed

- A draft bot sitting next to a person always picks with the draft model, instead of falling back to the pick order when a pack reached it mid-turn.
- Hovering a card in the sideboard step shows one card view, not two.
- Aiming a spell at a player or a card no longer flashes a second arrow at the target.
- On a phone, your hand stays in one row and swipes smoothly; it no longer jumps sideways as you scroll it.
- Dragging a card along your hand on a phone puts it where you let go, and holding it at either edge scrolls the hand.

## Fixes

- More end-to-end checks for the deck editor, the phone hand and targeting arrows, and steadier timing checks before an update ships.
- Card art rows for new test scenarios, and dependency updates.
- Planning notes for cardtable as a games platform.
