# v0.64.0 — A game log you can read

The game log is redesigned around turns, each trigger on the stack names the card behind it, and a
card you hover during a decision shows up right beside it.

## Changed

- **The game log is redesigned.** It reads in turns, each with both life totals, and you can jump
  back to any turn. A response sits under the spell it answered, what a spell did sits under it,
  the mana that paid shows as symbols on the cast, and combat is one block.
- A yes/no question with a lot of text wraps into balanced lines, so its box is closer to square.
- The Credits & attribution post is a plain list with a line thanking early helpers, and the front
  page's notes show credited names the same way.

## Fixed

- Each copy of a trigger on the stack points at the card it is for.
- A trigger from a lasting effect is shown as the card that made it.
- A card hovered during a decision enlarges beside the decision box, at a hand card's size.
- A challenge's AI-against-AI games finishing no longer restarts your own game.
- A room stops showing a game as in progress once the server lets go of it.
- A game's chat opens again when you come back after its table moved on.
- A spell's target is named in the log even after it moves, instead of "a card".
- The log no longer repeats every payment three times or says "untaps their permanents" every turn.
- A creature spell's log line says how it entered (a 5/5 with counters), not its printed stats.
- A spell with no legal target left says so in the log.
- The opponent's lines are marked as theirs again.
- A card a Signet can't actually pay for no longer lights up as playable, and is never spent twice.

## Fixes

- Test and record-keeping updates.
