# v0.50.0 — Your game follows you

Opening your game in another window moves you there, the front page's replay skips the dead air, and Rally's draft options read more clearly.

## Changed

- Opening your game in a second window moves you there instead of showing it to you as a spectator: the old window says the game is open elsewhere, and Play here brings it back.
- The replay on the front page skips the long pauses where nothing happens.
- A pack's fan moves only with the arrow keys; the wheel turns a card to its back face or tokens, and otherwise scrolls the page. (@ohmusama)
- In Rally, each set of a draft option shows its set symbol with its own Pack and Cards buttons. (@ohmusama)
- A release post now pings whoever reported a fix in it.

## Fixed

- The front page no longer features a game you're playing in, which could take your seat from the board you were playing on.
- The Discord button in the header opens a working invite again.
- A cube's or set's card list in Rally loads without jumping, and with no second scrollbar. (@ohmusama)
- The cursor in a replay, and the one a watcher sees for the bottom player, shows that player's name instead of a blank tag.
- A seat you take just after reloading or closing another tab is no longer given away a moment later.
