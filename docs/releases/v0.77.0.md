# v0.77.0 — The Library

Your decks and cubes live together in the Library, you can build a deck from scratch, and your art choices follow your cards.

## New

- **The Library.** One button on the front page opens your decks and your cubes, as tabs. Your decks come in three rows, starred, made your own and fresh from a draft, the one you played last first.
- Star any deck to keep it at the top. (@coffeetraveler)
- Every deck tile says when you last played it, or that you never have.
- Each cube says how many of your decks were drafted from it, and opens just those.
- One search box in the Library finds a deck by its name, a card, its cube or its set.
- **Build a deck from scratch.** New deck opens the deck builder on an empty deck: pick a format, search for cards and press them in, with basics from the land counters. Name it and it saves itself as you build, ready to play from a room's My decks. (@hehexd12345678910111213141516171)
- **Your art choices follow your cards.** A printing you picked, or "always use oldest art", draws that way on both screens and in the replay, and never changes your opponent's cards. A drafted deck keeps its drafted art while its tournament runs. (@coffeetraveler)
- A draft room has a new Bots play setting: off, the bots only fill the draft table and an odd player out gets the bye.

## Changed

- Decks and Inventory are now the Library's Decks and Cubes tabs; old links still work.
- A deck in a running tournament wears an "In tournament" chip and can't be renamed or deleted.
- A draft past eight players splits into even pods, so the bots spread across them (14 players are two pods of 7).
- Round 1 of a draft past eight players pairs you within your own pod.

## Fixed

- Every drafted deck shows its cover art again, including decks from sets and imported cubes.
- Button labels sit in the middle of their buttons instead of a little high.
- Drop-down menus look the same across the site, their arrow inset like their text.
- Auto Yield no longer presses an extra OK just after your Declare Blockers window.
- A cost you can't pay, like Mana Leak's, is still cancelled when you click just as it appears.

## Fixes

- Room settings run on shared code other games can use too.
- Games move to a backup server less often when nothing is wrong.
- Frame rate is now measured, so a slow day shows up.
- The guide for people writing their own games lives with the code.
- Safer updates to the shared packages.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Planning notes and replies from the other hadoku services.
- Dependency updates.
