# v0.75.1 — Every click lands

A spell you cast keeps its caster again, a second land click is no longer dropped, and replays keep their log and their players' current names.

## Fixed

- A replay names each player as they are called now, in the game log, its turn headers and on the stack, not by the name they had when the game was played.
- After End Turn, the spells that were lit stop being clickable once the turn starts passing, so a click there no longer flashes as a refused play a step later.
- Moving a replay's scrubber keeps the game log: it shows everything said up to that point instead of starting empty.
- Tapping lands for mana one right after another no longer ignores the second click, so a payment never stalls waiting on a land you already clicked.
- Casting a spell no longer sometimes leaves you holding priority over it, as if it were your opponent's, with its line missing from the game log.

## Fixes

- The board's and the other screens' animations run through one shared motion kit.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
- Planning notes.
