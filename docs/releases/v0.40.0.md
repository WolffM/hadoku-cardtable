# v0.40.0 — Clearer arrows and combat

Arrows are redrawn, combat reads blow by blow, and restored games come back whole.

## Changed

- **Arrows are redrawn and easy to see on every theme.** Each arrow is a tapered ribbon with an outline that stands out on light themes and busy art; while someone is still aiming it is dashed and moving, and it closes into a solid arrow once the target is chosen.
- A card targeted by something on the stack gets corner brackets, and the arrows and brackets of whatever resolves next gently pulse.
- A pile of tokens or creatures that attacks splits into its separate cards for the attack, and piles up again when combat ends.
- Combat damage to a player counts up as each attacker hits, ending on the life lost.
- Replays are off on the front page until they work properly.

## Fixed

- A game restored after a server restart comes back whole: no "pay 2 life?" for lands already out, no half-drawn board, and the right turn number.

## Fixes

- Game servers are no longer spun up and down for nothing during quiet stretches.
