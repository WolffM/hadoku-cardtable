# v0.60.0 — Replays with art

Every replay shows its cards' art, rooms sound when people come and go, and the front page's open seats look like the room.

## New

- A room now sounds when someone joins it, when someone readies up and when someone leaves.

## Changed

- The front page's "Open seats now" card shows the room's seats: a 1v1 draws the host's face against a "?" for the open seat, like its Discord preview, and a draft pod draws its seats in rows of four. Click an open seat to join.

## Fixed

- Replays show their cards' art, including ones your browser had saved before the art was fixed.

## Fixes

- Replays report how many cards drew their art, so a missing picture shows up in the logs.
