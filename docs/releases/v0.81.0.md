# v0.81.0 — Steadier boards and restarts

Restarts keep your tokens, clocks always flag, and boards, seats and drafts hold steady.

## Changed

- Restart turn is unavailable on a turn where a token copy of a card with abilities is in play.

## Fixed

- A board no longer misses an occasional update, like a hit left undrawn.
- A chess clock that runs out always ends the game on time, never freezing at 0:00.
- Picking a mode, cube or setting right after the page loads always takes.
- A draft's card question and the pack keep a whole card in view; the side column scrolls instead.
- Opening an invite to another game's room no longer shows "no such room".
- A heavy blow knocks the player's portrait back as it lands, not a moment after.
- Reloading twice about 30 seconds apart no longer gives up your seat at a table.
- A scried card no longer flashes on top of your library before Top or Bottom opens.
- A stack item both players drag at once no longer zigzags on a slow connection.
- Restarting a turn keeps your tokens' abilities: Saproling Burst's Saprolings keep their size, and a Treasure still makes mana. (@coffeetraveler)

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- More accurate reports when a game's buttons look stuck.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
- Planning notes.
