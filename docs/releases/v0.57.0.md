# v0.57.0 — A fair pick timer

The draft's pick timer is fair to the last second, and Cancel and Concede always do what they say.

## Changed

- When your pick timer runs out, you get the card you're hovering or have open, instead of the bot's choice.
- The pick timer's fuse burns fast at first, slows as it nears zero, and flares red in its last seconds.
- Your draft picks are grouped by mana value only, one pile per mana value with lands last.
- Draft rooms have their own chess clock switch, apart from the pick timer.
- The mana ring's colours no longer grow when you point at them, and the land under it stays put.

## Fixed

- The pick timer's fuse no longer runs out seconds before your pick is made.
- On phones the pick timer's fuse is a bar over the pack, and the pack no longer jumps.
- Cancel stops the game passing to the end of the turn at once, and repeated presses no longer hold up a concede.
- A click made just as the game passed a step no longer lands on the next decision.
- Conceding always ends the game.
- Cancel stays available while you tap lands for a cost you can't cover.
- Replays show the cursor where the player's pointer came to rest before picking a card up.
- A game that failed to start no longer freezes every other table on the server.
- Thoughtseize and Duress show the opponent's hand and your discard choice together every time. (@thyeggman)

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships: fairer timing checks, retries for flaky tests, and every test on its own free ports.
- The automated test player stops on a full server, and its reports say more about stuck concedes and refused clicks.
- Better logs for draft pick timeouts and dropped clicks.
- Card art rows for new test scenarios, and dependency updates.
