# v0.46.0 — Mill, power and life in motion

Milled cards fly, power and toughness changes show on the card, life totals land whole, and a round of draft, lobby and phone fixes.

## New

- **Milled cards fly into the graveyard.** Cards milled or surveiled from a library fly onto the pile one by one, face up, and a big mill riffles in about a second.
- **Power and toughness changes show on the card.** A comet runs along the border, up for a pump or an anthem and down for a shrink, and the P/T pill rolls to the new numbers. Battle cry runs down the line of attackers, and an effect wearing off at end of turn glints too.
- On a phone, hold a deck tile to peek at its list; lift your finger to close it.

## Changed

- **Life totals land whole.** Each hit, drain, lifelink gain or payment jumps the counter straight to the new total, with a punch in red or green. Each attacker, and each hit of a spell that hits more than once, lands as its own chunk.
- Every creature's P/T pill is easier to read, tinted green when buffed and red when shrunk.
- The draft's fan view uses the whole width, puts the pod beside your picks, and follows browser zoom.
- A format's set symbols sit on one row you swipe or drag.
- The front page gallery preloads its other pages' art, unless your browser saves data.

## Fixed

- The front page no longer grows as it finishes loading.
- Room cards no longer say "drafting" or "playing" twice.
- Switching a room's year or format no longer flashes the deck grid black.
- The draft pod no longer freezes for a second as it opens.
- A burst of triggers like Valakut's runs a little more smoothly.
- A tournament hub's table strip no longer draws the score over a name.
- The green ready ring around a seated player is no longer clipped on a phone.
- The standings table shows every header, and scrolls sideways on a phone.
- A password room's chat no longer shows before you join it.

## Fixes

- Fewer false alarms and needless restarts on the hosts that run matches.
- Counts of which screens and buttons get used and how much load the server carries, and a daily security report.
- Groundwork and test tools for playing on a phone held upright.
- Versions come from the release notes, and a finished one-off data migration is gone.
- Card art rows for new scenarios, and dependency updates.
- Planning, ledger and access docs.
