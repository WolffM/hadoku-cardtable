# v0.44.0 — Pick a draft in Rally

Rally forms can now ask which draft to play, and you are known by your account rather than your sign-in key.

## New

- **Rally forms can ask which draft to play.** An organizer offers draft presets, sets or cubes in a pick question, and each option opens its whole card list or a freshly opened sample pack, in fan or grid.

## Changed

- Rooms and drafts saved before this update are cleared once: you are now known by your account rather than your sign-in key, so a new key never makes you a stranger to your own room.
- Rooms and drafts no longer keep a copy of your name or character, only who you are, and show your current ones once you are back. After an update, someone who has not come back yet shows as "friend".

## Fixed

- With "Same target for all" ticked, the target popup no longer flashes for every remaining trigger, and an answered one no longer comes back.
- A trigger whose target you picked no longer sometimes asks its question again.
- Board wipes, mass deaths and creatures returning from the graveyard no longer stutter.

## Fixes

- Planning docs for showing player names from the account.
- Dependency updates.
- More draft and targeting tests, and card art rows for new scenarios.
