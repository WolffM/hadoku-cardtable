# v0.39.0 — Dice and coins on the table, and events on the calendar

Dice and coins land on the table, events go on the front page's calendar, and games keep going through a server restart.

## New

- **Dice roll on the table.** When a card rolls a die, the die tumbles out of the card, bounces across the felt and lands on the number you rolled, on both screens. Several dice tumble together, and one the roll sets aside is dimmed.
- **Coin flips on the table.** A card that flips a coin flips the same coin as the toss for who goes first, and when you called it, the coin is ringed to show whether you won the call.
- **Events on the calendar.** Create event, beside Create room, puts a draft pod, a tournament, a room or a meet-up on the front page's Upcoming card; its room shows as Scheduled with a countdown. Calendar lists every event, and the organiser can move or cancel their own.
- A die roll and a coin flipped mid-game each have their own sound.
- The front page's third card can show the last few messages from the Discord's #general, with a Join Discord button.

## Changed

- **One-of-each pickers show the kinds they need.** Sundering Titan, Cataclysm, Global Ruin, Shard Convergence and the like show each kind as a symbol that lights up as your picks cover it; ticking a card that crowds out an older pick unticks that one for you.
- **Watchers see the game from the active player's side.** Both hands are face up, and watchers are shown what the active player is shown: revealed cards, announced choices, dice and coins.
- Nothing happens after a die roll or coin flip until it has landed.
- The front page's third card shows one thing each visit: a room waiting for players, a cube spotlight with Play now!, the last game, or the Discord invite.
- "Open Discord", "Watch" and "Join" on the front page are buttons like "Read all" and "Calendar".
- "Read all" on the patch notes opens every release's full notes on the site.
- Rooms that don't match your filters fade darker and greyer.
- Hexproof, shroud and ward no longer flash a glint along the card's edge.
- In a Gauntlet, the loser of a game chooses play or draw for the next one.

## Fixed

- A server restart no longer ends the games being played, and no longer takes the server down again a few minutes later.
- A game moved after a server restart is moved once, and a finished game no longer comes back to life.
- A dropped connection no longer locks you out of your own game: your tab takes its seat straight back.
- Clicking a spell and pressing OK straight away no longer loses the spell.
- A creature's power and toughness always show, even turned sideways with several keywords.
- In a game against the AI, the AI's seat shows its picture and name instead of an open seat.
- A token keeps its own art when a game is restored after a restart.
- A replay no longer says "Connecting…" over the board.
- People watching a match see the dice and coins land, and the game log fills for them.
- Guided Passage no longer lets the opponent take Dryad Arbor as both the creature and the land.
- The coin toss at the start of a match no longer shows a drag grip on the coin.
- Smoother scrolling in the room list (most of all in Firefox) and in the stats and settings panels.
- Scrollbars match the rest of hadoku.
- Pop-up windows cover the whole window instead of sitting under the header.
- The front page's cards are no longer cut off on a phone.

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships: the e2e drivers act on the live window, and the full Forge run re-runs a red build instead of reporting it green.
- Game hosting: machines are added and released as load needs, a machine whose AI is cut short takes no new games, and only our own slices count.
- Testing rooms appear without waiting on the card catalog, and follow the room filters.
- A card-data download that fails partway no longer stops the server.
- Running the app locally needs no service key.
- Front-page scroll measurements.
- Card art entries for new test scenarios.
- Planning notes and the playtest ledger.
- Dependency updates.
