# v0.70.0 — Leaders, Discord events and fairer pods

Room leaders get more control, events reach Discord, and a draft pod pairs players before bots.

## New

- **Events on Discord.** Every event on the calendar also shows in the Discord server's Events tab, with a reminder in #events an hour before. Moving or cancelling it updates Discord too, and a room with a password stays off.
- **Leaders run their room.** A room's leader can remove a seated player, put the bot in their seat, drop a seat, and hand the room to anyone with the crown button. (@kawakimi)
- A lone creature blocking a menace attacker shows a broken-shield mark. (@kawakimi)

## Changed

- **The next game waits for you.** Between games of a series, the next game opens when you press Enter game, and its clock gives a one-minute grace. Sideboarding from the room opens the same full-screen panel as in game.
- A draft pod pairs its players with each other before anyone meets a bot.
- Auto Yield also answers Declare Blockers, going on with the blocks you set. (@the.scrubdaddy)
- The clock looks like a clock, so it no longer reads like your life total.
- Card state marks, like summoning sickness, are bigger. (@ohmusama)
- The room list shows the newest rooms first.

## Fixed

- Dragging a mana source moves it; its ring opens only on a click. (@kawakimi)
- Looking at an opponent's card no longer says "I have to wait for priority".
- The opponent's clock outline is red, like every other enemy marker.
- The game-over screen stops animating flyers, so it stays smooth.
- An ability on the stack shows its card's art for every card.
- Hovering the open card in a draft pack zooms it like any other card. (@kawakimi)
- The library viewer shows every card's cost and the deck's full colours.
- A drafted deck's match music follows every colour it plays.
- A draft event on the calendar names its pool, not a constructed format.
- Only a room's leader can seat or unseat the AI.
- An event you've joined no longer offers Sign up.
- A spectator's avatar decoration no longer shows under a room's name on the front page.
- A spell's targeting arrow ends when its target leaves for another zone.
- Leaving a draft pod mid-round no longer loses your deck for game 2.
- A tapped creature that dies breaks apart where it stood.
- A dragged stack effect follows the dragger's hand on the other screen.
- An ability chip no longer slides over a card your opponent just targeted.
- Every small bordered button highlights on hover the same way.

## Fixes

- Frame-rate reports from each game, to find what slows a board down.
- Each screen reports the stack drags it draws, to check two screens agree.
- Shared fixes for the other games on the platform.
- An admin test board for the latest play test's items.
- Steadier checks for drafts, pods, rooms and the automated players.
- Card art records for the cards new tests use.
- Clearer wording in the project guide, and updated planning notes.
- Dependency updates.
