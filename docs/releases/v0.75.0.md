# v0.75.0 — Cards that change the draft

Every card that changes a draft now works, game speed and a Display tab arrive, and round 1 is paired the moment a draft ends.

## New

- **Every card that changes a draft works.** Lore Seeker, Paliano, the High City, Regicide, Deal Broker, Canal Dredger, Archdemon of Paliano, Cogwork Spy, Spire Phantasm, Illusionary Informant, Whispergear Sneak, Leovold's Operative, Agent of Acquisitions, Cogwork Grinder, Animus of Predation, Noble Banneret, Paliano Vanguard, Smuggler Captain, Custodi Peacekeeper, Garbage Fire, Lurking Automaton, Pyretic Hunter, Cogwork Tracker and Aether Searcher each do what they say, from a face-up card you press with a pick to a question asked over your pack. (@thyeggman)
- What a card notes during the draft counts in your matches: Lurking Automaton and Pyretic Hunter enter with their counters, Garbage Fire and Custodi Peacekeeper use their number, and Aether Searcher finds its card. A bot's deck carries its notes too.
- What a card does to the draft in public (drafted face up, revealed, a pack added) is listed under the pod for everyone.
- A face-up card you can use at any time is offered while you wait for a pack too.
- **Game speed and a Display tab.** Game speed replaces Fast Mode: Normal, Fast (everything plays twice as quickly) or Instant (what Fast Mode was; if you had it on, you're on Instant). Display turns off card animations, screen shake, looping animations and the front page match, each on its own. (@coffeetraveler)
- The match on the front page has a pause button. (@coffeetraveler)
- Chat plays a soft sound when someone writes after a quiet minute. Turn it off under Settings, App, "Chat sound".
- A cube too small for everyone at the table shows an (i) saying how many cards will come round twice. (@ohmusama)

## Changed

- **Round 1 is paired the moment a draft ends.** Press Play when your deck is ready and you go straight to the bracket, with your match waiting for your opponent's deck. The empty "who is ready" page is gone.
- A question a card asks you during a draft is drawn over your pack, one at a time, and a long one scrolls.
- Settings are split into Gameplay, Display and Sound tabs. If your device asks for reduced motion, the motion settings start off.
- On your opponent's turn, the game stops at their Beginning of Combat when you can tap one of their creatures before it attacks, with a permanent (Master Decoy) or a spell in hand (Ice). (@rhomancer, @coffeetraveler)
- The pulse on End Turn and on the next spell to resolve is a soft ripple, and it no longer makes the game work harder while you decide.
- A draft pick does less work in your browser, so the next pack is drawn sooner.
- In a draft's fan, the mouse wheel steps through the pack when the pointer is not on a card. (@rhomancer)
- In the draft's side column the room's name has its own row, with (i), fan|grid and Close under it.

## Fixed

- Tapping a second land for mana right after the first no longer crashes the game back to the start of the turn. (@coffeetraveler)
- Restart turn draws the same card it did the first time, even after an upkeep shuffle (Land Tax). (@coffeetraveler)
- Karn Liberated's ultimate restarts the game for real, and the game log no longer shows each line twice afterwards. (@coffeetraveler)
- Full control: paying for Chromatic Star (or any sacrifice ability) with your lands no longer asks whether to sacrifice it.
- A blocker's arrow only snaps to an attacker it can legally block.
- A series' next game no longer tells the last game's loser that they won the coin toss: it says "Choose play or draw." (@rhomancer)
- A game restarted twice in quick succession keeps the cards you had been shown. (@ohmusama)
- A player who leaves a tournament partway through keeps their name in the standings, the results and the Tournaments tab.
- The deck builder no longer jumps down mid-build when another player finishes.
- The last card of a draft dragged to the sideboard is in the sideboard when deck building opens. (@ohmusama)
- A picked card flies to your pool over the pack, from the card you picked.
- A room switched from constructed to draft no longer sticks on "Checking…" in the pool picker.
- The featured match's title bar on the front page stays on one line.

## Fixes

- Frame rate and usage telemetry for the front page and the draft.
- A server failure on a claw now logs where the rules engine threw.
- Steadier, lighter checks before an update ships.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
- Dependency updates.
