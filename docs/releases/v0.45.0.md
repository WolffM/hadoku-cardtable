# v0.45.0 — Triggers one at a time

Chains of triggers resolve one by one, spectators keep their side, and Alchemy is gone.

## Changed

- **A chain of triggers resolves one at a time.** Ten Valakut triggers now land a short beat apart on both screens, even when nobody acts in between. Your own clicks are never held by it.
- **Spectators keep their side.** Watching a match, the table no longer turns over every turn; the switch between the two avatars turns it whenever you like. The front page's match card still follows whose turn it is.
- Alchemy sets and cards are gone from every set list, cube and search.
- A creature's hit knocks the player's avatar back instead of cracking the table.
- Decision cards are only as wide as their question.
- A Rally draft's cards are laid out a column per colour, by mana value.
- A Rally draft option shows its kind and a line per set or cube it drafts from.
- The front page's Discord chat scrolls back through recent history.
- The front page carries a small line saying Card Table is an unofficial fan project.

## Fixed

- A creature's damage and death crack wait for the fireball's hit to land.
- A countered spell's crack and a banned deck's red X no longer flash early.
- A board full of flyers no longer slows down loop after loop.
- Bots in big rooms each wear a different character.
- Hovering a deck the moment the deck grid appears opens its list.
- The front page's cube spotlight art loads with the page.
- Hovering a card in a Rally list or sample pack enlarges it, and the wheel turns a double-faced card.
- A form made from Create event shows under Polls straight away.

## Fixes

- More tests for trigger chains, flyers and member accounts, and room chat ready for new members.
- Safer card data downloads, and saved tournaments moved onto accounts.
- Card art rows for new scenarios.
- Planning, ledger and legal docs.
