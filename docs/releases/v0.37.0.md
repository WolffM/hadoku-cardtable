# v0.37.0 — Weight, replays and a new front page

Cards land and spells hit with real weight, every game is recorded, and the lobby is rebuilt.

## New

- **Creatures land with weight.** A big arrival shakes the table, throws up dust and cracks the
  board, scaled to its size. Reanimated creatures rise from the graveyard in shadow, and blinked
  ones flicker back in.
- **A new front page.** The busiest live game plays at the top, beside the latest patch notes,
  upcoming events and a rotating card. With nothing live, it plays back the latest game.
- **Replays.** Every game is recorded and can be played back with pause, speed and a scrubber.
- **Move any pop-up out of your way.** Drag a question, a payment, an opened graveyard or your deck
  list anywhere on the table. The next question opens where you last put one.
- **Cards about to go glow red.** Choosing a legend to keep, a creature to sacrifice or a target to
  destroy or exile lights the card that would leave.
- **Your character says what it got wrong.** A second land, acting without priority or casting
  without enough mana gets a speech bubble only you see. Turn it off in Settings → Game.
- An "All" button sends every legal attacker at once, and each arrow can still be re-aimed.

## Changed

- **Spell damage hits harder.** A bolt whips in on a wind-up arc trailing fire, and a bigger hit
  throws a bigger ball, explosion and number, up to twenty damage. Big blows shake the table too.
- **Room cards, redesigned.** Each room is coloured by its format, with a mark for draft or duel,
  its seats, mode and what it's doing now. Filter by type, tournament or "Not full".
- Formats read "Standard 2018" or "Modern 2011" instead of a raw date.
- Artifacts, enchantments and planeswalkers you cast land with weight by mana value.
- Bigger creatures land with heavier thuds; planeswalker attacks have their own impact.
- A spell hitting several targets is heard once per target.
- A countered or fizzled spell cracks and fades on the stack.
- The two sides of the table meet as one, with a little room at the seam.
- Fact or Fiction's piles show as card groups with a button each.
- Searches like Gifts Ungiven pick the whole set in one window, greying cards that break the rule.
- With an attack tax or upkeep cost coming, the game stops so you can crack a fetchland first.
- Done Attacking and Done Blocking appear only while you declare.
- Cards no longer pop up browser tooltips repeating what they show.
- When several games start at once, each table shows its place in the queue.
- The GitHub repo is linked beside Discord in the top bar.

## Fixed

- No more "you may pay" asks you can't afford, like Punishing Fire without red.
- An attack tax you can't pay no longer locks the game.
- Paying through a Signet no longer cancels the spell.
- Calibrated Blast and other reveal-until cards show what they revealed.
- Lifelink gains life as the creature hits, not before.
- Infernal Tutor only glows when you're actually Hellbent.
- Ability chips name the card instead of "CARDNAME".
- Hovering a venture card shows its dungeons; a planeswalker shows its emblem.
- Hovered tokens show their power and toughness.
- A card played into an empty row waits in its own spot until it lands.
- A hovered card's stats and counters rise with it.
- A flyer's attacking outline floats with its art.
- Targets on the battlefield are no longer covered by the decision window.
- Every Gauntlet game flips its own coin, and its last game waits for both players.
- An empty seat is given back after 30 seconds without a connection.
- A renamed player shows their new name in rooms they had joined.
- The Custom tab names each pack straight away.
- Set drafts of Bloomburrow, Duskmourn and 24 other sets start again.
- A game whose engine stops ends with a message on both screens.
- The server no longer goes down at start-up when Forge is slow.
- The settings panel fits the screen at any UI scale.
- Smoother play with a busy board, a deep stack or a big board wipe.
- Decision windows have the same border as every other window, in Firefox too.
- Long room names are cut off, and room status lines up with its detail.
- The lobby no longer scrolls past the window.

## Fixes

- Groundwork for hosting many games per engine across more machines, with failover and alerts.
- Each game's engine only accepts its own players.
- The computer opponent stops thinking when a game ends and decides attacks faster.
- Groundwork for rebuilding replays from each game's moves.
- A testing room for Gifts Ungiven, and card art entries for new test scenarios.
- Load tests, fault tests and a nightly fuzzer that plays random moves.
- Steadier checks before an update ships, and alerts when a full run fails.
- Planning notes, hosting test results and requests to the fleet's manager.
- The patch notes' last section is called Fixes.
- Dependency updates.
