# v0.54.0 — Aim with a click

Attackers and blockers aim with a click and an arrow, draft bots pick better cards, Mox Opal's colour can be backed out of, and updates bring you back in about two seconds.

## Changed

- **Attackers and blockers aim with a click.** Click your attacker or blocker to pick it up, and an arrow follows your pointer; click a player, planeswalker or attacker to send it there, or click it again for the old one-click action. A drag still works too, Escape or a right-click puts it down, and on a phone a tap works as before.

- Draft bots pick better cards: they now judge a card by how highly cube drafters rate it, not by its rarity, and the AI's deck built from a bot's picks plays its best cards first. (@thyeggman)

## Fixed

- After an update that brings new card rules, the site no longer stays down for about twenty seconds. A match you're in says "Updating…" until it's back.
- A mana source that asks its colour after tapping, like Mox Opal, untaps again when you back out of the colour (Escape or a click away).

## Fixes

- A server stopping for an update now finishes cleanly within the time it's given.
- Steadier checks before an update ships, and tooling for the trailer, the desktop tour and the sound review.
- A study of running CubeCobra's draft bot on our server.
- Card art for new test scenarios and decks.
