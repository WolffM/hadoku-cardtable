# v0.61.0 — Challenge rooms

Replay a real MTGO Challenge against its own field, see each past tournament's podium, and play with any name.

## New

- **Challenge rooms: replay a real MTGO Challenge.** Create room → Challenge, pick the event, its top 8 or its full field, and which player you are; you play their list, and every other seat is an AI playing the list its own player played. The first two are the Legacy and Modern Challenge 32 of 2026-02-07.
- Each past tournament on the Tournaments page shows its top three on a podium beside the standings, rising as you scroll to it.

## Changed

- A tournament one person played against the AI no longer shows on the Tournaments page.
- The music review takes a note per track, and an admin's picks go live when they submit.
- A creature you send into the attack shows its arrow to the player again; one click still sends it.

## Fixed

- A player whose name has brackets in it, like "name (nick)", no longer has their whole turn skipped.
- Names with non-English letters (like 王蟲様) show correctly in matches instead of as garbled symbols.
- A 1v1 room seats you in the seat you click, lets you move to the other one, and offers the AI on either open seat.
- In a Classic tournament, an AI you were paired against plays the deck the tournament locked for it.
- Revealed cards close on a spectator's screen and the front page's watch card when they close for the player.
- Jumping into a replay while something is being revealed shows the reveal.
- Double-clicking a ban in a Gauntlet no longer adds a scroll bar to the room.
- A Discord, Steam or uploaded picture on the match board is cropped to a circle instead of showing as a square.

## Fixes

- Room game types come from one registry, ready for games other than Magic.
- The shared service kit is published as its own package.
- Art rows for newly placed cards, and dependency updates.
- A targeting arrow with no position no longer logs an error to the browser console.
- Planning and sound docs.
