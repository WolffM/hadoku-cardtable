# v0.49.0 — A word before you waste mana

Passing with mana floating asks first, a new setting stops at every end step, and replays of a series you are still playing stay hidden until it is over.

## New

- Passing with mana still floating asks first, and Untap takes back the lands you tapped for it. (@the.scrubdaddy)
- A setting to always stop at the opponent's end step, even when you have nothing to play. (@the.scrubdaddy)

## Changed

- The front page's wildcard card no longer has a "Last game" page; replays are on the featured match already.

## Fixed

- A game you are still playing out (game 1 of a Bo3, or a game a Rematch can still continue) no longer shows up in your replays with both hands face up; you can watch it once the series is over.
- On a phone, a tap straight after a quick drag counts again, so a blocker flicked onto an attacker can be tapped back out of the block.
- Your own seat's ready pill says ready or not ready, like everyone else's, and changes when you press it. (@the.scrubdaddy)
- The phase list beside the board no longer draws a second, unstyled list on top of itself.
- In Firefox, room cards and glows no longer show a speck of stray pixels at their centre.

## Fixes

- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Card art for new test scenarios and decks.
- Notes for the project board.
