## New

- **Stats is a page of its own now.** The trophy opens a Leaderboard: everyone's rankings with how far each rating moved in the last 30 days (sort them by rating, win rate, wins or games), the decks and colours that win most, and every rated match, newest first. Your profile has your rank and rating, a chart of your last 30 matches, your unfinished games, your recent matches, how you do against each player, and your decks and colours.

## Changed

- Find any player from the box at the top of the Leaderboard, or click a name to open their profile.
- The Decks table on a profile filters by format, room and deck name with the same chips as before, now drawn as pills.
