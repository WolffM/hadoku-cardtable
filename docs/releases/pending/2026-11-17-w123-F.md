## Changed

- Between games of a series, the next game opens with a press — "Enter game" — instead of
  jumping to it by itself; the clock gives both of you a quiet minute before it starts running.
- Sideboarding from the room, between games of your own series, now opens the same full-screen
  sideboard panel the in-game screen draws — not the room's lobby with the panel squeezed in
  below the fold.
