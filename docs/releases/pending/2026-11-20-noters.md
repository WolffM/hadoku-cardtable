## New

- Custodi Peacekeeper, Garbage Fire, Lurking Automaton and Pyretic Hunter work in a draft: everyone is told when you draft one, and it notes how many cards you have taken that round.
- Cogwork Tracker is revealed and notes who passed it to you. Aether Searcher is revealed, and so is the next card you draft, which it notes.
- What your cards noted during the draft counts in your matches: Lurking Automaton and Pyretic Hunter enter with their counters, Garbage Fire and Custodi Peacekeeper use their number, and Aether Searcher finds its card. A bot's deck carries its notes too.
