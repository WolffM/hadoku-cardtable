## New

- **Creatures and other permanents land with weight.** A big creature's arrival now shakes the
  table, throws up dust and rock, and cracks the board under it, all scaled to its power — and a
  land-sized landing is just a drop. Reanimating a creature flies it up out of the graveyard in a
  pall of shadow and takes its colour back; blinking one in from exile flickers it into place. A
  token made during a spell's resolution now materialises right over its own spot instead of
  flying in from across the table. Non-creature permanents — artifacts, enchantments,
  planeswalkers, battles — land the same way, scaled to their mana value.
