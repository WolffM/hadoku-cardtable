## New

- Settings has a Display tab where you can turn off card animations, screen shake, looping animations and the front page match, each on its own.
- The match on the front page has a pause button.
- Game speed replaces Fast Mode: Normal, Fast (everything plays twice as quickly) or Instant (what Fast Mode was). If you had Fast Mode on, you're on Instant.

## Changed

- Settings are now split into Gameplay, Display and Sound tabs.
- If your device asks for reduced motion, card animations, screen shake, looping animations and the front page match all start off until you change them.
