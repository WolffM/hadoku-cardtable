## New

- **A room link unfurls in Discord.** Paste a room's address and it shows the room's name, what it plays (like "Modern Gauntlet 1v1"), and your avatar against an open "You?" seat, or against whoever has sat down.

## Changed

- A room's address is now `hadoku.me/cardtable/room/<id>`. Old links with `#/room/` still open the room.
