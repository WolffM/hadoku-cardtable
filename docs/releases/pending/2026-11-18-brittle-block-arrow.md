## Changed

- A lone blocker on a menace attacker now has a cracked, faded block arrow with a hollow head that shakes now and then, on both players' screens. Add a second blocker and both arrows turn solid. This replaces the broken-shield mark in the card's corner.
- Attacking and blocking creatures no longer wear the crossed-swords and shield chips in their corner. Their outlines, the attacker's tilt and the combat arrows show the same thing.
