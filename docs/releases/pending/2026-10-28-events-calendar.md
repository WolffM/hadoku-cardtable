## New

- **Events on the calendar.** Create event, beside Create room, puts a draft pod, a constructed tournament, any room or a casual meet-up on the front page's Upcoming card. A pod, tournament or room event opens its room straight away with you leading it, and the room shows as Scheduled with a countdown until it starts.
- Calendar on the Upcoming card lists every event with when it starts, what it is and who organised it; the organiser can move or cancel their own.
