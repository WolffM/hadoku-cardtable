## Changed

- When your pick timer runs out, you get the card you're hovering or have open, instead of the bot's choice.
- The pick timer's fuse burns fast at first and slows as it nears zero, and flares red in its last seconds.
- Your picks during a draft are grouped by mana value only, one pile per mana value with lands last, instead of by colour.
- Draft rooms have their own chess clock switch, apart from the pick timer, so a draft or a tournament can run either, both or neither.

## Fixed

- The pick timer's fuse no longer runs out seconds before your pick is actually made.
- On phones the pick timer's fuse now shows as a bar over the pack, and the pack no longer jumps when it appears.
