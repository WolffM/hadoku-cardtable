## Fixed

- A mana source's ring no longer pops up when you're trying to drag the card — it only opens on a
  release that never crossed the drag slop, so dragging and rearranging your lands and creatures
  works the way it should.
- Clicking an opponent's permanent or graveyard card to look at it no longer shows "I have to wait
  for priority" — that line is reserved for an actual attempt to act with your own card.
