## Fixed

- A creature exiled "until this leaves the battlefield" (Banisher Priest and the like) now comes back when it should after a game is restarted from its turn's start or moved to another server mid-game. Before, it stayed in exile for good.
- A turn holding an effect that a restart cannot yet put back exactly (such as "+1/+1 until your next turn") can no longer be restarted, rather than being restarted without it.
- After a game is restarted from its turn's start or moved to another server mid-game, a player with hexproof from a permanent (Leyline of Sanctity and the like) can no longer be targeted. Before, the hexproof still showed but did not work.
