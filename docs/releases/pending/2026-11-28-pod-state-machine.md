## Fixed

- A drafter who leaves the room mid-draft is out of the event from round 1: their opponent takes the win instead of waiting at an empty table.
- Closing your tab for a while during a round no longer gives away your seat at your table: it is still yours when you come back, and nobody else can sit in it.
- Swiss rounds with bots in the field no longer pair the same two players again while someone new is free: two humans in a pod of bots play each other once, then the bots.
- A drafter who leaves the room mid-draft no longer freezes the draft for everyone else: their seat picks for itself from then on.
- In a room split into two pods, the second pod now uses the room's pick timer and bot setting too.
