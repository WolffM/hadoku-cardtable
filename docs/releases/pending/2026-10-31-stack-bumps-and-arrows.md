## Changed

- Cards and ability chips on the stack now bump into each other and spread out in slightly different directions, overlapping at their edges, and pile on top of each other when the board is full instead of shrinking.
- The stack never covers End Turn and the buttons beside it.
- Arrows between cards that sit close together on the stack now arc over them, long enough to see which way they point.
- The corner brackets show only while you are choosing a target, not after it is chosen.
