## Changed

- **Spell damage scales further, and looks the part.** A bolt now flies a wind-up arc — pulled back
  and out to one side before it whips in — instead of the same gentle curve every time, and leaves a
  trail of fire behind it. A bigger hit throws a bigger ball, a bigger explosion, and a bigger number,
  all the way up to twenty damage (creature combat scales the same way too); the explosion itself
  throws more fire the heavier the blow, rather than one big blob. A spell's own blow shakes the table
  too, gentler than a creature's. A Calibrated Blast's bolt now flies from the card it revealed, not
  from nowhere.
