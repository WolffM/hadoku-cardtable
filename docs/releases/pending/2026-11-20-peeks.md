## New

- Cogwork Spy works in a draft. Everyone is told when you draft it, and you alone are shown the next card drafted from that pack and who took it.
- Spire Phantasm works in a draft. As you draft it you guess which of the cards left in the pack is drafted next; that card is then revealed to the table, and a right guess counts for the Phantasm in your matches.
- Illusionary Informant works in a draft. It lies face up until you press its name and choose a player: it turns face down and you alone are shown the next card they draft.
- Whispergear Sneak works in a draft. It lies face up until you press its name and choose a pack nobody is looking at, by player: it turns face down and you alone are shown that pack.
- A face-up card you can use at any time is offered while you wait for a pack too.
