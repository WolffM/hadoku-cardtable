## New

- **A podium when a tournament ends.** First, second and third stand on their medal blocks with their characters, the winner crowned, under falling confetti.

## Changed

- **The tournament bracket is a Swiss bracket.** Each match sits on the row of its players' record, lines step winners up and losers down, and a Final column lists everyone by place. Press a player to light their path through the rounds.
- Match cards in a tournament room show both players, their faces and games won, and never squeeze; a live match still takes you into its game.
- Standings show rank, player, record and points. Tie-breakers opens the tie-breaker columns, with the one that separated players level on points marked.
- Nobody watching is listed under a tournament's matches any more: the chat shows who is in the room.

## Fixed

- A tournament under way no longer offers its format, year, mode, Tournament switch or room settings to change: they are read-only from the deal on, instead of answering a press with an error.
