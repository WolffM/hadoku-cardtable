## New

- The room leader can reset a tournament game: it starts again from scratch with the same decks, the match score unchanged, and both players are taken back into it.
- The room leader can disqualify a player from a tournament: their current match counts as a loss, whoever they would have played afterwards gets a bye, and they stay in the standings marked Dropped.

## Changed

- Leaving a tournament room mid-round now counts as dropping out: your opponent wins the match at once, and their game ends on the win instead of waiting on an empty seat.
