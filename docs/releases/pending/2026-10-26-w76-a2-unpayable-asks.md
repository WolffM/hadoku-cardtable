## Fixed
- A "you may pay" you have no mana for is no longer put to you: two Punishing Fires in the graveyard no longer ask for {R} when you have no red.
- Attacking into Elephant Grass (or any attack tax) you can't pay no longer locks the game: that creature simply doesn't attack.

## Changed
- With an attack tax or your own upkeep cost coming and a fetchland ready, the game now stops at your Beginning of Combat (or on the upkeep trigger) so you can crack it before paying.
