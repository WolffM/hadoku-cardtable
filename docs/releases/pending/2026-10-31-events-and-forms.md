## New

- **Create event makes a Tournament or a Form.** A tournament takes all of its room's settings when you create it: game type, mode, format or cube, deck rule, chess clock, pick timer and hand smoothing. A form is a Rally sign-up form: create one on Rally and come straight back with it attached, or paste the link of one you already have.

## Changed

- The calendar always shows what an event's room is set to now, so a change made in the room shows there straight away.
