## New

- **Drag a card out of the pack to take it.** Let it go over your picks to take it, or over the sideboard to take it straight into your sideboard; let it go anywhere else and it goes back. Clicking and Enter work as before.
- Drag the fan sideways to scroll through the pack.
- An (i) button on the pack lists the draft's controls.

## Changed

- The fan/grid switch moved from under the pod to the room's bar, beside Close and the new (i) button.
