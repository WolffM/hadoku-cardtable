## New

- Leovold's Operative works in a draft: press its name, then two cards, to take both. It turns face down and your next pack passes on without a pick (you are shown it).
- Agent of Acquisitions works in a draft: press its name, then any card, to take the whole pack. For the rest of that round every pack passed to you goes straight on, and you are shown each one.
- Cogwork Grinder works in a draft: press its name with any pick to remove that card from the draft. The table is told a card was removed, not which.
- Animus of Predation works in a draft: press its name with any pick to remove that card from the draft, shown to everyone.
- Noble Banneret and Paliano Vanguard work in a draft: press the name with a creature you pick to reveal it and note its name (Banneret) or its creature types (Vanguard).
- Smuggler Captain works in a draft: press its name with any pick to reveal that card and note its name.
