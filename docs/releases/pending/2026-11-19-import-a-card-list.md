## New

- **A cube from a card list.** The draft room's Import box takes a pasted list as well as a Cube Cobra link: one card a line, or a Cube Cobra export. Name it and it is saved to your Inventory like any import.

## Changed

- An imported cube becomes the room's cube at once: cards Forge can't play are named right away, and "Draft without them" deals the rest.
- A link to any of a cube's Cube Cobra pages imports it, not only its overview and list pages.

## Fixed

- Inventory names who imported a cube instead of showing an id.
- Inventory offers Delete on your own imports.
