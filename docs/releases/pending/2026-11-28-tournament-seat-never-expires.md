## Changed

- Your tournament seat is yours until you leave the tournament: closing the tab, a reload, a lost connection or a server restart never costs you your draft seat or your table, however long you are away.
- Mid-tournament, Leave reads **Leave tournament** and asks once before it drops you; Main menu, the top bar and Back just take you away.

## Fixed

- A leader can disqualify a drafter during the draft; the draft bot takes their seat at once.
- A drafter who left the tournament and came back into the room no longer gets their seat back from the bot.
