## Changed

- The front page's game card is now a square close-up of the bottom-left of the board: the avatar, life, graveyard and the near half of the hand and battlefield, readable instead of a shrunken whole board.
- The card always shows the table from the side of the player whose turn it is, turning around at every turn.

## New

- Replays show the active player's cursor and their drags as they happened (games played from this release on).
- When the active player leaves their cursor parked elsewhere on the table, reading or away, the card glides over to follow it, and back to their corner once they return.
