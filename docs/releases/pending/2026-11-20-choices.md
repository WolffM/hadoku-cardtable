## New

- Paliano, the High City and Regicide work in a draft. Everyone is told when you draft one, then the player to your right, you, and the player to your left each choose a colour, and the three are listed for the table.
- Deal Broker works in a draft. It is drafted face up, and after the last pick you may reveal a card of your pool; every other player may offer you one of theirs, which only you see, and you may accept one offer to swap the two cards before deck building.

## Changed

- A question a card asks you during a draft is drawn over the top of your pack, one at a time, and a question that offers many cards scrolls.
