## New

- **The Library.** One button on the front page opens your decks and your cubes, as tabs.
  Your decks come in three rows: the ones you starred, the ones you made your own, and the ones a draft made that you have not touched yet, each with the one you played last first.
- Star any deck to keep it at the top.
- Every deck tile says when you last played it, or that you never have.
- Each cube says how many of your decks were drafted from it, and opens just those.

## Changed

- Decks and Inventory are now the Library's Decks and Cubes tabs; old links to either still work.
- A drafted deck whose tournament is still running wears an "In tournament" chip and can't be renamed or deleted until it ends.
