## New

- **The front page's Upcoming card is now Calendar and Polls.** Calendar lists every upcoming event with View and Sign up; Polls lists the open sign-up forms with Open. Flip between them with the arrows, the dots or a swipe.

## Changed

- The front page's wildcard card can be flipped through again: it opens on one pick and you can page to the rest.
- Organizers move or cancel their own events right in the calendar list.

## Fixed

- The front page's replay starts at the deal, showing the player's hand, instead of an empty "Turn 0" board.
- In a replay, the card a player is dragging is shown face up instead of as a card back.
