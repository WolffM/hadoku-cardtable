## Changed

- **One-of-each pickers show the kinds they need.** Sundering Titan, Cataclysm, Global Ruin, Shard Convergence and the like show each kind as a symbol that lights up as your picks cover it, and each ticked card shows what it counts for. You never say what a card is for: ticking a card that crowds out an older pick unticks that one for you.

## Fixed

- Guided Passage no longer lets the opponent take Dryad Arbor as both the creature and the land while another land could be chosen.
