## Changed

- A Classic room's year is a slider now, not a dropdown: a ruler of years, with longer marks every five and ten years. Drag the year along it, tap anywhere on it, or nudge it a year with ‹ and › or the arrow keys. The deck grid changes once you let go.
- Free pick's year browse is the same slider, with "All" at its left end.
- Players who aren't leading read the room's format plainly, e.g. "Standard 2015".
