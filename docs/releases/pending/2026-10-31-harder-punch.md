## Changed

- Creatures wind up before they hit a player: each draws back first, then slams into the avatar much faster. The bigger the creature, the deeper and slower the wind-up and the harder the hit.
- After the hit, small creatures spring straight back, while big ones linger on the blow and lumber home.
- When several creatures attack together they hit one after another, weakest first and the strongest last, each curving in on its own path.
- A big creature's hit on a player shakes the table, cracks it and knocks nearby cards aside, just like a big creature landing.
