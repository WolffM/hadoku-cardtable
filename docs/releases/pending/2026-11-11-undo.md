## New

- **Undo takes back your last action: a land, a cast or an activation.** It sits beside Restart turn and asks your opponent first; the AI always allows. It reaches back to when your opponent last had priority, and a draw, a reveal or anything random ends it.

## Changed

- Forge's own "Undo (N)" no longer appears beside Resolve. A tapped land still untaps when you click it.
