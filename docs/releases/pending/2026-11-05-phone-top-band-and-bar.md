## Changed

- **On a phone held upright, the game has a new top band and one bottom bar.** Both players sit in the top band with their life, name, hand count and graveyard, exile and library buttons, and poison, energy, experience, the monarch or the initiative and floating mana hang under each one. The bar holds the button for whatever you're deciding, or a line saying who you're waiting for; Chat, Menu and zoom are behind ⋯.
- On a phone, your hand rests as a strip of card tops and rises when you tap it, so the board gets about 130px more room. Tap the board to drop the hand again.
- On a phone, an empty row of the board folds away, a row with only a few cards draws them bigger, and a crowded row of creatures wraps onto two lines.
- On a phone, after you pinch to zoom in, you can drag the board up and down to see all of it.

## Fixed

- On a phone, tapping a graveyard or exile count now opens that pile.
- On a phone, swiping the board while choosing a target no longer cancels the spell.
