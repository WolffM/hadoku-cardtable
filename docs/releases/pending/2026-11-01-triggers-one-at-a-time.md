## Changed

- A chain of triggers that resolves with nobody acting in between (ten Valakut triggers, say) now resolves one at a time, a short beat apart, on both screens. Your own clicks are never held by it.

## Fixed

- A creature's damage and its death crack now appear when the fireball that deals it lands, not the moment the spell resolves.
- A countered spell's crack and a banned Gauntlet deck's red X no longer show part of themselves before they start drawing.
