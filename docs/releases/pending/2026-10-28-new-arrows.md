## Changed

- **Arrows are redrawn and easy to see on every theme.** Each arrow is now a tapered ribbon with an outline, so it stands out on light themes and over busy card art, and its tip lands just inside the card it points at.
- While you or your opponent are still aiming, the arrow is dashed and moving; once the target is chosen it closes into a solid arrow.
- A card targeted by something on the stack gets corner brackets, and the arrows and brackets of whatever resolves next gently pulse.
