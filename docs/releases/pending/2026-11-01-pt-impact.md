## New

- When a creature's power or toughness changes, a comet of light runs along its card's border, up for a pump or an anthem and down for a shrink, and the numbers on its P/T pill roll over to the new ones. Battle cry runs down the line of attackers, and an effect wearing off at end of turn glints too.

## Changed

- Every creature's P/T pill is easier to see over the card art, so it reads as always there, and a buffed or shrunk creature's pill is tinted green or red.
