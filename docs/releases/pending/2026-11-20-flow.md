## New

- Canal Dredger works in a draft. It is drafted face up, and from then on the last card of every pack is passed to you instead of round the table. If several players drafted one, each of them keeps their own last card and everyone else is asked which of them to pass it to.
- Archdemon of Paliano works in a draft. It is drafted face up, and while it is you cannot see your packs: each is one button that drafts a card at random. After three cards it turns face down and you see your packs again. A second Archdemon drafted along the way asks for three cards of its own.
