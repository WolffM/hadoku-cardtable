## Changed

- A card in your hand whose cost has changed shows what it costs now over the printed cost at the top of the card, where it used to sit above the power and toughness.
- A land with more than one ability besides its mana asks which one in a list that spells each ability out, where the mana circle showed them as identical buttons.

## Fixed

- A card revealed on top of a library no longer shows through the open game log.
- Opening or folding a turn in the game log scrolls it into view when its lines would end below the panel.
- A card you can play from your graveyard is outlined once, on the card, where the whole pile used to be outlined behind it as well.
