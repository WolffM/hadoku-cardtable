# v0.73.0 — You open the door

Every game is now entered by your own press, from a table page that is one doorway, and a batch of board fixes from the last playtest.

## Changed

- **A table's page is now a doorway.** It shows the two players, the score and one button that says where it takes you: Enter game, Return to game, Sideboard or Watch. Every game is entered by your own press; nothing walks you onto the board any more.
- Play on a drafted deck takes you to the room's page, where your own match lights up on the bracket when it is ready.
- Sideboarding between games is always the full-screen step, in draft pods too.
- Your clock's one-minute grace at the start of a game ends when you arrive at the game.
- A room's Spectators list names only the people in the room now, not everyone who ever visited it.
- A tournament or draft room's link preview shows everyone seated in it, and calls a tournament a Tournament, not 1v1.
- A card in your hand whose cost has changed shows the new cost over the printed one at the top of the card.
- A land with several abilities besides its mana asks which one in a list that spells each out.

## Fixed

- Offspring, Casualty and Conspire ask whether you pay again. (@rhomancer)
- A card you may cast with mana of any type, like the one Cruelclaw's Heist takes, can be cast when your lands are the wrong colour.
- A card you played no longer stays lifted over the board after the game has finished with it.
- Your character says "I don't have enough mana for that" only for something you tried to play, not for a cost an opponent's spell asks.
- The table page no longer squeezes the sideboard into a small box, or shows a game card beside an Enter game panel.
- The button into your game no longer depends on how you reached the page or whether you reloaded it.
- Watch on the bracket goes straight to the game.
- A card revealed on top of a library no longer shows through the open game log.
- Opening or folding a turn in the game log scrolls it into view.
- A card you can play from your graveyard is outlined once, not twice.

## Fixes

- Groundwork for more games on the same table.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Art for the cards the new checks play.
- Dependency updates.
