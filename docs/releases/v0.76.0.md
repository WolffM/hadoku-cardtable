# v0.76.0 — Friends-only rooms

Friends can make rooms only friends can see, a guest browser's work follows you to your account, and finished tournaments clear out after a day.

## New

- **Signing in to your account from a guest browser keeps what you made as the guest.** Its decks, rooms, parked games, presets, imported cubes and tournament results move to your account instead of being lost.
- **Friends-only rooms.** Friends can switch on Friends only when they create a room, and nobody below friend tier can see or join it. (@cozmiverse)

## Changed

- A finished tournament leaves the rooms list a day after its last round, and the front page's results card shows for a day instead of a week. Its results stay on the Tournaments page.

## Fixed

- A busy server no longer lights up cards you can't play, like a counterspell with nothing on the stack.
- Ctrl+wheel zoom in the deck editor keeps the exact spot under the pointer.
- A land under its open mana ring keeps its lift while you move onto the colours.
- The settings gear's Account tab shows a member as Member, and offers Delete account again.
- An event's sign-up list keeps everyone's face and name when the account lookup is slow.

## Fixes

- Reduced motion from your system's setting takes effect from the first frame.
- The shared game packages publish complete again.
- Steadier checks before an update ships.
- Dependency updates.
