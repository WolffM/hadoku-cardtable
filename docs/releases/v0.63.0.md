# v0.63.0 — Credits where they're due

The release notes now thank the people behind each fix, and a room's people list matches
everywhere you look.

## New

- **A Credits & attribution post under every release's notes.** It thanks everyone who reported a
  bug or asked for a feature, most credits first, and the people who helped before there were
  notes. Past releases now credit who asked for each fix too.

## Changed

- A credit in the release notes shows your name instead of pinging you.
- A room's people list and chat come straight from Card Table, so names and faces match everywhere.

## Fixed

- A new name shows straight away in everyone's room list.
- Someone with the same room open in two tabs counts once.
