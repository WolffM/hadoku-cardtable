## §F — Forge fork: lands are tappable (R15)

Forge's clickable filter excludes every mana ability: `forge-ai/src/main/java/forge/ai/AvailableActions.java:82`
(`!sa.isManaAbility()`), so a land is never offered at priority and cannot be tapped for mana before
casting. Desktop Forge lets a player tap lands freely.

1. A real-Forge test first (`lands.jvm.test.ts`): at your main phase with an untapped land, the land
   is in the offered set; clicking it adds its mana to your pool; nothing else changes. Fails on the
   current jar.
2. Change the fork so a permanent with an activatable mana ability is offered while the seat has
   priority. Keep a mana ability that costs more than tapping (or has a side effect) out unless it is
   cheap to prove right; say what you chose.
3. Build to `~/.cache/cardtable-w35/` with the toolchain in `~/.local/opt`; push the fork branch;
   bump the gitlink without initialising the submodule (`git update-index --add --cacheinfo
   160000,<sha>,forge`). The manager asks the operator to install the jar.

**Done when:** the test fails on the installed jar and passes on the new one; the fork diff is that
file (and its test helpers) only; jar sha256 in the note.
