## Operator rulings, 2026-10-02 — settled

- **R7, casting follows the MTG Arena model.** Operator: *"in mtg arena the card hovers to follow the
  mouse for a bit until its over your battle field, then just the arrow follows the mouse as you go to
  your target. clicking can also make the card follow your mouse so that seems the same as drag to
  me."* So: pressing OR clicking a castable card lifts it and it follows the pointer; once the pointer
  is over your battlefield the card settles there (the staging spot) and from then on **only the
  targeting arrow follows the pointer**; releasing or clicking on a legal target answers the target
  question; Escape or a drop back on the hand cancels cleanly. A card without targets is cast by
  releasing it over the battlefield. Click and drag are ONE interaction, not two.
- **R4, Thoughtseize is one step.** Operator: *"i think this can be consolidated to a single step. the
  opponent doesnt need to confirm to see the card that was taken that's silly."* The caster gets one
  choice showing the opponent's whole hand with the unchoosable cards greyed (the Rampant Growth shape);
  **nobody gets a confirmation afterwards**: the opponent learns which card went from the log and their
  graveyard. An animation is a later item.
- **R1, chat collapse and resize are watchparty's.** Filed as `docs/requests/WATCHPARTY_CHAT_COLLAPSE.md`;
  no lane.
- **R16, the result screen has two buttons.** Operator: *"'rematch' button should take the users back to
  the room where decks are to be selected. there should also be a button to take you back to 'main
  menu' where the lobbies are. if the previous leader selects 'main menu' and the other person selects
  'rematch' then the other person becomes the new room leader."* So: **Rematch** returns you to the
  same room's deck selection, with both players un-readied and the finished match no longer holding the
  room; **Main menu** leaves the room (you stop being a member) and goes to the lobby list; when the
  leader leaves, leadership passes to a member who stays. A room left by everyone is no longer listed.

## §R — Rooms: leave, leader handoff, rematch (R16)

Today a room has **no leave**: `apps/server/src/room/lobby.ts` only ever adds members. And a room keeps
its finished match's id while that match sits in `over` (ten minutes, `overGraceMs`), so the room page
would send a "Rematch" straight back to the old match.

1. **Leave.** A member can leave a room: removed from `members`, their deck hold and ready flag dropped,
   persisted. **If the leader leaves, leadership passes to the member who joined earliest among those
   remaining** (operator ruling above). A room with no members left is not listed (the stale-room rule
   already hides rooms; make sure an empty one is hidden at once).
2. **Rematch.** When a room's match is over (or gone), the room releases it: `matchId` cleared and every
   member un-readied, so both players land on deck selection and ready up again. Decide whether this
   happens when the match ends (server-side, on the match's own end) or when the room is next read, and
   say which; either way the old match cannot pull a returning player back.
3. Shape 5 (the route and the client method), with server tests: leave as a member, leave as the leader
   (the other member becomes leader), the last member leaves (room hidden), a finished match no longer
   holds the room. Each fails when reverted, with counts.
