## §G — the drag (V4, V5, V2)

1. **Release-time only.** Delete the `lifted`→`settled` transition (`install.ts:572-573`). On release,
   apply the Settled rules:
   - `selectCard` for a play;
   - for a targeted card: the cast, then the arrow from where the card parked.

   Keep the `window` listeners without pointer capture (`install.ts` header). jsdom and
   `arrow.spec.ts`'s `pointercancel`-on-`window` depend on that.
2. **The type-line rule** reads `BoardCard.card.typeLine` (the wire's current `Type`), as
   `isOwnBattlefieldLand` does. Instant, Sorcery or an `Aura` subtype is targeted; anything else is
   no-target.
3. **No-target cards:**
   - over the viewer's own battlefield: play, at the slot under the release point (ONE placement call,
     at release);
   - elsewhere: float back to the hand slot (CSS transform, about 300 ms, `prefers-reduced-motion`
     respected) and send nothing.
4. **Targeted cards** (outside the hand): the cast goes out at release, the card parks there, and the
   arrow (`arrow.ts`) follows the pointer and snaps to legal targets. A click answers through
   `commit.ts`.
5. **Cancel (V2):** right-click or Escape while Forge is asking about the cast (a selection is open, or a
   payment) sends Forge's Cancel, and the card floats back. With no question open, nothing is sent; this
   is the regression `ae1a236` fixed, and it stays fixed.
   - **"Cancel on the first choice":** verify a targeted cast and a modal spell each show Forge's own
     Cancel at their first question. If one does not, that is a prompts change (not owned): write it up,
     don't edit.
6. **Click (option A):** a press with no movement lifts the card; the next press is the release, at its
   own point.
7. **The cues:**
   - the battlefield lights up for a no-target card;
   - the hand shows a cancel cue under the pointer;
   - a label on the card says "let go: play it", "let go: back to hand" or "let go: choose a target".

   CSS in `board.css` or `gestures.css`, through the `--z-*` scale.
8. **Delete** everything the settle needed (Settled list). Rewrite R9's placement as the single release
   call.
9. **Unit tests**, one per Settled rule, plus:
