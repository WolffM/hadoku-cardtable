## Settled — do not re-open in any lane (operator, 2026-11-01)

1. **Portrait only.** A phone held upright gets the new layout. Desktop and landscape keep today's board, unchanged.
   The switch:
   - Condition: viewport ≤ 600 CSS px wide AND taller than wide.
   - It sets `document.documentElement.dataset.layout = 'portrait'`.
   - Everything portrait is styled under `:root[data-layout="portrait"]`.
   - No other breakpoint, and no user-agent sniffing.
2. **Players and phases go on top**, where the opponent's hand was.
   - **Player chips:** one chip per player, in the same row, carrying avatar, name, life, hand count, library count
     and a graveyard button. The opponent is on the left, you on the right.
   - **Turn colour:** the active player's chip carries the turn colour.
   - **Stack:** a count chip between the two player chips, shown only while the stack is non-empty. Tapping it opens
     the stack list. Identical items group into one row with ×N.
   - **Phase bar:** horizontal, below the chips. It shows 5 segments (Begin, Main, Combat, Main 2, End), with the
     current one widened to show its step name and ticks for its sub-steps.
3. **The board is four lanes, top to bottom:** their lands, their creatures and other permanents, your creatures and
   other permanents, your lands.
   - **Width:** at most 3 cards across at normal zoom.
   - **Scrolling:** each lane scrolls sideways on its own. A sliver of the next card, an edge fade and a "+N" count show
     there is more.
   - **Lands:** a cropped name-and-art strip, with identical lands grouped as ×N (today's `copies`).
   - **Tapped:** a tapped creature turns 90°; a tapped land dims and tilts.
   - **No page sideways scroll:** the page body never scrolls sideways. Only lanes do.
4. **Zoom:**
   - Pinch scales the board's card size (minimum = 3 per row, maximum ≈ 2.4×).
   - Double-tap on empty table toggles between 1× and ~1.7× at that point.
   - Lanes stay scrollable at every zoom.
   - A "%" button in the action bar shows the zoom and returns it to 1×.
5. **A tap reads; it never plays (touch only).**
   - A touch tap on any card, including the opponent's and hand cards, opens the one expanded card view (memory
     `one-expanded-card-view`), with that card's legal actions as buttons (Cast, Play land, Activate, …).
   - Dragging a hand card up past the hand's edge plays it.
   - A mouse keeps today's behaviour exactly, so desktop is unchanged.
6. **Decisions dock over the hand and action bar, never over the board.**
   - Targets stay visible and are chosen by tapping them on the board, including the player chips.
   - **The expand button (and swipe-up to full) appears ONLY when the decision's content does not fit the docked card**
     (operator, 2026-11-01: "decisions like 'declare attackers' don't need a fullscreen expand button"). The full
     sheet is for long content such as library searches, and it has a pinned Done.
   - Swipe down or "–" shrinks a decision to a pill; tapping the pill restores it.
   - **Decision bodies scroll vertically only** (operator: "horizontal scroll bar on decisions is bad").
   - While a decision is open, a tap chooses, and a long-press opens the expanded view.
7. **The action bar** sits between board and hand, and holds:
   - chat (with an unread badge)
   - the menu (Game log, End turn, Full control, Concede with in-place confirm)
   - the zoom pill
   - the primary button (Pass / next step / Resolve)

   In portrait during a match, the room's chat column is closed and reachable only from the bar.
8. **Card borders are thin** (operator: "the borders around the cards is a bit big"). Use the prototype's thin frame,
   applied to real card art.
9. **One board, not two.** Portrait is the existing `Board` / `LiveBoard` rearranged by CSS plus a few
   portrait-only wrappers, with the same test IDs. Never a second board component and never a copied render path
   (memory `waves-must-delete-what-they-replace`).
10. **Out of scope:** landscape phones, tablets, the draft and room screens, the front page (wzoom holds
    `apps/ui/src/front/**`).
