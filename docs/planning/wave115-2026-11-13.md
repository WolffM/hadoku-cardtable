## The goal, and how it's proven

**Done-when:** `packages/game-server` has a `Lobby` core that creates, joins, seats, readies, persists, restores,
lists, sweeps and closes rooms for **any registered game type**, through a per-type hook interface. A test proves
it with **no Magic import**: it registers a toy game type with its own `game` blob, then runs a room through the
platform alone:
1. create, two members join and take seats, ready, and a watcher;
2. leave hands leadership to the earliest joiner (ruling 6);
3. persist, restore on a fresh lobby, and get the same room;
4. sweep.

Magic's lobby is the same core with Magic's hooks.
