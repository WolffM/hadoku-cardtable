  and gain health."* The design the operator approved ("you understood the assignment") is the canvas
  https://claude.ai/artifact/BZGfndqa4MeDCDrDwDdCvn — four artboards: damage to a player, life gain (with
  stacking), damage on a creature (survives / dies), and the 700 ms timeline. Its rules are restated in
  Settled below so a lane needs no link. The wire carries `Life` per player (`adapter.ts:1042`) and

  visualize cards in exile with counters on them."* Counters are drawn by `board-card__counters`
  (`Board.tsx:1703`) on battlefield cards only; the exile pile (`Board.tsx:2675`) draws faces alone.
- **AB1.** *"some loaded quick, but others loaded extremely slowly. are these relying on external
  requests? this is a static list we should be able to cache these images."* `DeckArt`
  (`ConstructedScreen.tsx:666`) sets each option's `<img src>` to `cardImageUrl(artId)`
  (`apps/ui/src/draft/cardImage.ts:72`, the art host via `packages/carddata/src/art/index.ts`); nothing
  prefetches, and the browser fetches 12–20 images cold per era on first open.

## Settled — do not re-open in any lane

- **Theme tokens only. No invented colours** (operator, 2026-10-07: *"lets avoid inventing our own colors
  wherever possible"*): damage in `--danger` / `--danger-ink`, gain in `--success` / `--success-ink`, the
  struck-through old value in `--ink-faint`, the number's outline stroke in `--bg`. The theme has every
  token the design needs; a lane that believes it needs a new one stops and reports.
- **The numbers, as designed.** A number spawns WHERE the change is: a player's life at that seat's
  face, a creature's damage at its P/T corner. It rises 28 px over 700 ms (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2,
  1)`), opacity 1 → 0 over the last 280 ms; the life counter turns the colour, ticks in 150 ms steps and
  keeps the old value struck through for 1.5 s; simultaneous changes offset 12 px sideways and start 80
  ms apart; a change of 5 or more pops to 1.25× for 90 ms first; damage that stays on a creature is a
  pill beside the P/T until cleanup; a creature that dies gets its number, the fade to the graveyard
  starting at the number's halfway point; nothing for 0. Reduced motion: no rise, no pop, fade only.
  Fast Mode: the counter ticks at once, the number still rises. Drawn in the overlay root (like
  `presence.*`), from view deltas, never from a click; the other seat sees the same at mirrored
  positions. No sound this wave.
- **No explanatory UI text.** A counter is a pill with Forge's counter name and count, the same
  `board-card__counter` drawing the battlefield uses; the exile pile's top card shows it, a hover shows
  the rest.
- **Deck art:** when a room opens the deck list, the art of every option in the chosen era is requested
  at once with `<link rel="prefetch">` (or an `Image()` warm-up), and the picker draws from cache; the
  art host and URLs do not change (a card's art is a lookup, `art-manifest.json`, never a hardcoded host).
  No image bytes in this repo.
- Delete what you replace (`made dead / deleted: …` per note). Comments explain the code; ids and lane
  letters go in commit messages only.
- Proof on the claws: `git push origin HEAD:refs/heads/verify/w56-<lane>` then
  `gh workflow run forge.yml -R WolffM/hadoku-cardtable --ref verify/w56-<lane> -f only=e2e -f specs='…'`;
  never `--ref main`; delete the branch after. Work dirs on disk: `$HOME/.cache/cardtable-play-work/w56-<lane>`;
  local e2e ports **49xxx** (`CARDTABLE_E2E_SERVER_PORT=49008 CARDTABLE_E2E_PLAY_SERVER_PORT=49028
  CARDTABLE_E2E_EDGE_PORT=49108 CARDTABLE_E2E_PLAY_EDGE_PORT=49128`). A plain `pnpm exec vitest run <path>`
  needs no jar env and no lock.
- Landing: `scripts/land.sh` from the wave worktree after each verified lane; CI is the gate; closing notes
  landed, then `touch docs/handoff/w56.READY`. Evidence is text under `docs/planning/wave56-evidence/<lane>/`
  (no PNG under `docs/`).

## §N — floating numbers (AB12)

1. `numbers.ts`: subscribe to the match (as `presence.ts` does), diff `life` per player and `damage` per
   battlefield creature between views, and spawn a number element in the overlay root at the anchor
   (`[data-player-face][data-player-id]` for a seat, the card's `.board-card__pt` for a creature), with the
   motion, stacking, pop, counter tick and reduced-motion rules from Settled. Pure helpers for the diff and
   the layout, unit-tested in `numbers.test.ts` (jsdom: elements, attributes, timings by fake timers).
2. The life counter in the seat bar carries the colour and the struck-through old value for 1.5 s
   (`Board.tsx` anchor attributes; the drawing in `numbers.css`).
3. `normalizedBoardText` in `play.spec.ts` hides the numbers layer the way it hides `.presence`.
4. Real Forge, `e2e/w56-numbers.spec.ts` on the claws (3/3, 0 SKIPPED): a Lightning Bolt to the face
   spawns `−3` at the opponent's face on BOTH screens; a Healing Salve spawns `+3` in the gain colour; a
   blocked attacker's damage spawns at the P/T corner; table-fit's every-frame check stays green (run it).
5. Mutation: drop the diff → the spawn test FAILS; drop the stagger → the stacking test FAILS (counts).
6. Done when: the pins fail when reverted, the spec and table-fit are green on a claw, and
   `docs/planning/wave56-evidence/N/numbers.md` records the computed colours (the token values), the
   timings observed, and the anchors used.

## §X — counters in exile (AB10)

4. Mutation: drop the counters → both FAIL (counts).
5. Done when: the pins fail when reverted and the spec is green on a claw.

## §D — deck art ahead of the picker (AB1)

1. When the deck list for an era is shown, request every option's art at once (prefetch links or an
   `Image()` warm-up, de-duplicated across eras), so the picker's `<img>`s resolve from cache; a failed

2. `constructed.test.ts`: opening an era issues one prefetch per distinct art, none twice; the options

3. Evidence: `docs/planning/wave56-evidence/D/art.md` — from a real browser (Playwright on the built UI):

4. Mutation: drop the prefetch → the test FAILS (counts).
5. Done when: the pin fails when reverted and the evidence shows every option's art painted within one
