- **The stack's layout** (operator, 2026-10-05, demo v5 approved: https://claude.ai/artifact/7gHwzveqpi4EiZKdVTJ6EX):
  - **Place:** the existing stack overlay column, the same place as today (x760, y92, w308 in the 1280×800
    spec, `docs/planning/board-layout-2026-09-27.md`), beside the phase rail. There is no empty middle band.
  - **Order:** a row of thumbnails, the top of the stack LEFTMOST, resolving left to right. They are
    numbered 1..n from the left, with an owner strip at the bottom (blue for mine, amber for theirs).
  - **The large view is the card image alone,** upright, directly under the row, with no side panel and no
    coloured outline. The only blue ring is on the selected thumbnail.
  - **One spacing value:** the gap between thumbnails equals the gap from the row down to the card. Measure
    from the FILLED row (the demo once measured it empty and overlapped).
  - **Never cover what matters:**
    - The card is pinned only while its box intersects no permanent and no open question panel.
    - Otherwise it is hidden, and hovering or focusing a thumbnail shows it one gap below that thumbnail.
    - Re-check on resize and on every board change.
    - Clicking a thumbnail while pinned shows that item.
- **Every arrow is one curve** (`arrowShape` in `gestures/arrow.ts`, `ea22a5d`): a quadratic bow, always on the
  same rotational side, with the head along the tangent. No other arrow geometry may exist. A counterspell's arrow
  arcs from its thumbnail to the targeted spell's thumbnail.
- **Attacking by drag** (X1): drop an attacker on an opponent's face, planeswalker or battle. The defender's

- **Declared attacks stay drawn:** once declared, each attacker has a passive arrow to its defender until combat
