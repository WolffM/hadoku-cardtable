## Why

Stage 3 (moving the UI's Magic to `games/magic/ui`) needs the room view to be generic. Today `lobby.ts`'s view
builder puts Magic's `game: MagicRoomGame` **and a top-level `ai`** on every room, bomberman's included. The
platform schema already says `game` is the game's own part, validated by the game. The server just doesn't ask
the game for it.

## Settled
