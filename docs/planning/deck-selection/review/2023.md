# 2023 — Standard (2023-05-29)

Format in force **2023-05-29 → 2023-09-08** (Standard had no rotation this year — WotC moved it to a three-year rotation and the autumn set dropped nothing — so the era is the format in force on August 1st). Sets: MID, VOW, NEO, SNC, DMU, BRO, ONE, MOM, MAT. Banned: Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Invoke Despair, Reckoner Bankbuster, The Meathook Massacre.

**Balance of the chosen decks.** Colours (decks playing each): White 12, Blue 11, Black 11, Red 8, Green 8. Styles: aggro 10, midrange 7, control 7, combo 0.

- Aggro dominated this era’s candidate pool (51% of its decks) and was given extra slots (10 of 24 chosen).

**The candidate pool’s own shares** (before selection; 39 legal decks): colours White 23, Blue 17, Black 20, Red 12, Green 13. Styles: aggro 20, midrange 10, control 9, combo 0.
