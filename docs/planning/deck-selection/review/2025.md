# 2025 — Standard (2025-06-30)

Format in force **2025-06-30 → 2025-08-01** (the last Standard before the fall rotation). Sets: DMU, BRO, ONE, MOM, MAT, WOE, LCI, MKM, OTJ, BIG, BLB, DSK, FDN, DFT, TDM, FIN. Banned: Abuelo's Awakening, Cori-Steel Cutter, Heartfire Hero, Hopeless Nightmare, Monstrous Rage, This Town Ain't Big Enough, Up the Beanstalk.

**Balance of the chosen decks.** Colours (decks playing each): White 13, Blue 9, Black 13, Red 10, Green 10. Styles: aggro 10, midrange 6, control 7, combo 1.

- Aggro dominated this era’s candidate pool (59% of its decks) and was given extra slots (10 of 24 chosen).

**The candidate pool’s own shares** (before selection; 49 legal decks): colours White 28, Blue 20, Black 24, Red 19, Green 22. Styles: aggro 29, midrange 9, control 10, combo 1.
