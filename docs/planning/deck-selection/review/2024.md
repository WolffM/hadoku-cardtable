**Balance of the chosen decks.** Colours (decks playing each): White 11, Blue 12, Black 13, Red 9, Green 10. Styles: aggro 10, midrange 6, control 7, combo 1.

- Aggro dominated this era’s candidate pool (47% of its decks) and was given extra slots (10 of 24 chosen).

**The candidate pool’s own shares** (before selection; 58 legal decks): colours White 27, Blue 28, Black 31, Red 20, Green 23. Styles: aggro 27, midrange 13, control 17, combo 1.
