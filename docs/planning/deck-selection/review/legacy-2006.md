**Landstill (Noah Long) was originally chosen, then dropped** (not transcribed, not in `decks/legacy-2006/`):
mtgtop8's own `.dec` export for this deck (id 265151) plays `8 [IA] Brainstorm` — Legacy limits
any non-basic-land card to 4 copies, and the export totals 62 maindeck cards. This is a defect in
mtgtop8's stored decklist, not a Forge transcription failure or a fixable card/set typo, so it is
not something this library's tools can correct without inventing a decklist. 2006's candidate pool
had exactly 8 legal, non-duplicate decks and all 8 were originally chosen, so there was no
alternate to promote in its place — 2006 is a **7-deck era**, not a padded 8. The full reasoning is
recorded machine-readably as `swapped_out_reason` on this entry in
`docs/planning/deck-selection/selections/selection-legacy-2006.json`'s `alternates` array, following
wave 34's own precedent for an untranscribable mtgtop8 export (see `decks/README.md`'s "Three
decks the selection chose could not be transcribed").
