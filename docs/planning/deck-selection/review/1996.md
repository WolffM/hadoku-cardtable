# 1996 — Type 2 (ALL)

Format in force **1996-07-10 → 1996-10-01** (Type 2 had no autumn rotation this year, so the era is the format in force on August 1st, when Worlds was played). Sets: FEM, 4ED, ICE, CHR, HML, ALL. Banned: Amulet of Quoz, Bronze Tablet, Channel, Jeweled Bird, Mind Twist, Rebirth, Tempest Efreet, Timmerian Fiends. Restricted (one copy): Balance, Black Vise, Ivory Tower, Land Tax, Zuran Orb.

**Balance of the chosen decks.** Colours (decks playing each): White 5, Blue 1, Black 5, Red 6, Green 4. Styles: aggro 5, midrange 5, control 2, combo 0.

- Red dominated this era’s candidate pool (29% of its colour pips) and was given extra slots (6 of 12 chosen).
