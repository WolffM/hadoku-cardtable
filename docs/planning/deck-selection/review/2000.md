# 2000 — Standard (PCY)

Format in force **2000-07-01 → 2000-11-01** (the last Standard before the fall rotation). Sets: USG, ULG, 6ED, UDS, MMQ, NMS, PCY. Banned: Fluctuator, Memory Jar, Time Spiral, Tolarian Academy, Windfall.

**Balance of the chosen decks.** Colours (decks playing each): White 5, Blue 5, Black 3, Red 5, Green 5. Styles: aggro 3, midrange 10, control 3, combo 0.

- Midrange dominated this era’s candidate pool (62% of its decks) and was given extra slots (10 of 16 chosen).

**The candidate pool’s own shares** (before selection; 16 legal decks): colours White 5, Blue 5, Black 3, Red 5, Green 5. Styles: aggro 3, midrange 10, control 3, combo 0.
