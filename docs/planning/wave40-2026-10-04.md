## §A — a cast stays hidden until it is on the stack (V3)

1. **Measure first.** With real Forge (a scenario: one seat casts a targeted spell from hand, such as
   Lightning Bolt in `bolt-player.state`), record what the OTHER seat's socket receives from the
   cast's first ask until the spell is on the stack: card objects, zone lists, names, `sourceCard`,
   stack items, log lines.
   - Read Forge's own order (`forge-game/.../player/PlaySpellAbility.java`, the zone moves and the
     rollback ≈:120-130) to know when the card leaves the hand.
   - Write the timeline in `A-timeline.md`. If the other seat learns nothing until the stack, V3 is
     already true: say so with the evidence and stop.
2. Otherwise, in `visibility.ts`'s per-seat projection, withhold from every other seat what a cast in
   progress reveals: the card object's face, and its arrival on the stack. Keep it withheld until the
   spell is actually on the stack, then release it in the same frame. On a cancel, the card goes back to
   hand unseen.
   - **Never by timing:** decide it from Forge's own state, the same way the projection decides
     everything else.
3. Run the server isolation unit tests and `visibility.test.ts`, plus a new `cast-hidden.jvm.test.ts`:
   - the other seat sees nothing between the first ask and the stack;
   - it sees the spell on the stack;
   - it sees nothing at all on a cancel.

   Mutation-test it.
4. Grep `e2e/` for readers (blast radius). Put any e2e diff in `A-readers.md` for the w39 manager.
5. **Done when:** `A-timeline.md` exists, the jvm test passes 3/3 and fails with the change reverted,
   `pnpm run check` exits 0, and the report quotes the operator's V3 sentence.
