# Wave 34 — the escape hatch works, nothing shipped is untested, and nine real eras

Protocol: `docs/handoff/w34.md` carries it. **Five lanes, all dispatched at t=0.**
Implementers commit and stop; the manager verifies from artifacts and lands lane by lane,
**in ONE push at the end**.

**Why.** Wave 33 built scripted Forge positions and, with them, found a real bug: **with Full
Control on and an opponent's spell on the stack, clicking your own instant does nothing**
(`docs/planning/wave33-evidence/e-findings.md` §E1). Full Control is the operator's stated escape
hatch for every stop the defaults skip, so it must work. It also found Ponder's follow-up decisions
being answered with no click (§E2). And four things shipped with no test that exercises them: the
order-of-triggers rule (Q8), the Choose X slider, poison counters, and the rail-row fix. This wave
closes all six. **And the deck library is replaced** (lane D): the operator, 2026-10-02, *"naw please get
deck-library integration now"*, ahead of reviewing the selection.

  those.
- **No jar install by a lane.** If §E1 or §E2 needs a Forge change, the lane stops and reports; the

- **The eras are the nine "power summers", 2008–2016, and the decks are the ones the selection chose**
  (`docs/planning/deck-selection/`: `eras.json`, `selections/selection-<year>.json`, the rules in
  `review/README.md`). Operator, 2026-10-01: no duplicate decks, colour and style spread, one era per
  year just before the fall rotation. Do not re-select by hand. A deck that cannot be transcribed or
  is not legal at the pin is replaced by the NEXT alternate in its selection file, and the swap is
  listed in the note.
- **Defaults the operator has not ruled on (they may overrule after landing):** all nine eras ship,
  including the thin 2009 (4 decks) and 2010 (8); 12 decks per era; the default era for a new room is
  the newest (`newestFormat()`), i.e. 2016.

## §S — Server play (§E1, §E2, Q8, two scenarios)

1. **§E1 — Full Control blocks your own response.** Repro: `lone-spell.state`, bob casts Memnite, alice
   turns Full Control on, clicks Mana Leak: nothing (no Pay Mana Cost, stack stays 1, a generic
   `getAbilityToPlay` ask surfaces ~15 s later on its own schedule). With Full Control off the same click
   casts. Find where the click is lost (the client's offer, `match.ts`'s act path, the bridge, or
   Forge's `YieldController` with `FULL_CONTROL_POLICY`). Write a real-Forge test that reproduces it
   first, then fix the cause. Evidence `wave34-evidence/s1-full-control.md`. If the cause is in the
   browser half, hand it to G with the evidence.
2. **§E2 — Ponder resolves with no decision.** `ponder.state`: after the cast, no ask reaches the browser
   and the stack empties. Something answers the look-at-three / reorder / shuffle decisions: find what
   (a server auto-answer in `auto.ts`, `match.ts`'s auto path, or Forge's own default), with the asks it
   answered logged. A choice that changes the game state is the player's; only a decision with exactly
   one legal answer may be auto-answered. If the auto-answer is in the browser (`prompts/rules.ts`),
   hand it to G. Evidence `wave34-evidence/s2-ponder.md`.
3. **Q8 — the ordering ask, exercised.** w33 could not reach it: a scenario's phase jump never
   materialises Upkeep triggers. Drive the game to Upkeep instead (a loop that passes until Upkeep and
   reads the stack before answering), with two simultaneous triggers. Real-Forge test: no ordering ask
   without Full Control; one with it. Revert the rule: the first fails.
4. **Two scenarios for G's and B's items:** `choose-x.state` (a spell with X in hand and a known amount
   of mana) and `poison.state` (a seat with poison and energy counters). Add their README rows.

**Done when:** `pnpm check` exits 0; each fix has a real-Forge test that fails reverted and passes
restored (counts); both evidence files exist.

## §D — Deck library: nine power-summer eras (DL1–DL4)

Today the picker's eras are exactly the files in `decks/pools/` (`apps/server/src/http/library.ts:74`
lists every `standard-<date>.generated.json` as a format), which is why the operator saw nine eras from
one 18-month window, including `2011-07-01`, a ban-only row that is a TEST fixture
(`build-pool-fixture.ts`'s header). The 49 decks are mid-season collections with up to four copies of
one archetype.

1. **The eras.** One per year, 2008–2016, from `docs/planning/deck-selection/eras.json` (each the Forge
   Standard format in force just before the fall rotation; `start` is its `Effective` date). Generate
   their pools with `build-pool-fixture.ts` against the real corpus (`--cards` the main checkout's
   `packages/carddata/data/cards.jsonl`, read-only). **Only the nine eras are offered as formats.** A
   test fixture pool (the 2011-07-01 ban path, or whatever replaces it) must not reach the picker:
   move it out of `decks/pools/` or make the loader offer only catalogued eras, and say which.
2. **The decks.** The `chosen` lists in `selections/selection-<year>.json` (96 decks), transcribed with
   `build-library.ts` from mtgtop8's `.dec` export (deck id and event id are in the selection). Each
   file carries its source URL, the era's format and `Effective` date, and the finish it had. The old
   eight collections and their 49 decks are removed; `decks/README.md` and `catalog.ts` describe the
   new library and point at the selection's rules.
3. **Legal and playable at the pin.** `validate-library.ts` passes against the corpus, AND every card
   in every deck resolves in **Forge's own card database at the pinned jar** (a jar-gated test, one
   file, using `CARDTABLE_FORGE_RES`). mtgtop8 set codes Forge does not know are the likely failure;
   fix the spelling in the transcription, never by editing a Forge file. A deck that still fails is
   swapped for its next alternate.
4. **Art.** Regenerate `apps/ui/src/draft/art-ids.json` with `scripts/art/build-art-ids.ts` so every
   card in the new library has an art id. Say how many cards gained one and how many have none.
5. **What pins the old library**, found by grep, all yours: `apps/server/src/http/{gate,library,routes,
