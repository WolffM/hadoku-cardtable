## Why

From the operator's live match `m-6ce49e226aef`, 2026-10-06:

- **The Island (ledger W10, "planned (engine)" since w40, never built).** *"i have an island open, and i can tap it.
  after tapping it i can only undo using the 'undo' button i can't click to untap anymore. after undoing it's not
  tappable anymore."* Cause, read by the tracker in the fork:
  - `PlayerControllerHuman.chooseSpellAbilityToPlay` (`:1664`) computes `cachedActionableCards` once per priority.
  - Tapping for mana uses no stack, so priority returns at once and the cache is recomputed with the Island tapped.
  - `tryUndoLastAction` (`:2697`) untaps it and re-shows the prompt, but `pushActionableCards` (`:1572`)
    re-publishes the **stale cache**. The Island stays unclickable until priority moves on.
- **Azorius Signet.** *"azorius signet is not tappable at all. something isn't working right here. i even added mana
  to my pool."* Cause, read by the other cardtable session in the fork:
  - `forge-ai/src/main/java/forge/ai/AvailableActions.java:92-110` (`cardHasActionable`) offers a mana ability only
    when `isTapOnlyManaAbility` (cost exactly `CostTap`). The Signet is `Cost$ 1 T`
    (`res/cardsfolder/a/azorius_signet.txt`), so it is never in the priority-time set.
  - Live evidence from `/tmp/cardtable-play/spare-0f8c0daefb22/stream.jsonl`, seat 1: Signet id 94 sat untapped
    beside an untapped Island, and the priority window's `setWeaklySelectable` named only the Island. Across the
    match the Signet was named in 4 payment-mode sets and 0 priority sets.

- **Augur of Skulls' upkeep ability (A).** *"i have a creature (Augur of Skulls) that has 'Sacrifice .... Play this
  ability only during your upkeep' i would expect the shortcutter not to skip past my upkeep if i have an active
  ability in play that requires upkeep."* Cause: `apps/server/src/play/auto.ts` `DEFAULT_STOP_POLICY`
  `skipNonStopPhases: 'always'` (:419), the operator's own wave-28 rule; see the leak argument at :36-48, where a
  stop conditioned on what a seat HOLDS reveals its hand. This ask does not reopen that rule. Augur is on the
  BATTLEFIELD (public), and its ability is `ActivationPhases$ Upkeep | PlayerTurn$ True`
  (`res/cardsfolder/a/augur_of_skulls.txt`). So the rule is narrow: **stop in a non-stop phase when a permanent the
  seat controls has an ability activatable now that is RESTRICTED to this phase.** "Any actionable permanent" is
  wrong, because Augur's `{1}{B}: regenerate` is actionable everywhere and would bring back stopping everywhere.
  Live evidence: the match's server log says `auto-passed seat 0 "Upkeep step"` every turn.
- **The redundant sacrifice ask (B).** *"after selecting the creature, and then selecting the sacrifice ability it
  AGAIN asks me if i want to sacrifice the augur of skulls. thats a redundant ask."* Cause: `StopPolicy.costConfirm`
  (`auto.ts:263-276`) is `'ask'`. Forge's cost confirm (`HumanCostDecision.java:1295-1310` → `confirmPayment` →
  `InputConfirm`) reaches us as a `showPromptMessage` with `card: null` plus a Yes/No bar, with the cost part and the
  ability dropped, so it cannot be told apart from an optional-trigger confirm.

(A and B reported through the other cardtable session, 2026-10-06, with the evidence above.)
