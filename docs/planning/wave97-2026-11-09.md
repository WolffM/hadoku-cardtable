**Why:** phase 4 of the platform plan (v2 at `~/.cache/cardtable-manager/handoff-2026-11-07/platform-plan-v2.html`, lines
620–634). w96 published the core in watchparty's `rooms-*` 0.45.61:

## Settled — do not re-open

- **Room logic is identical across apps; only CSS and UI differ.**
- **Ready is per userId.** The leader moves only on Leave, to the earliest-joined member, never on a disconnect. A
  spectator never takes an active seat. Nobody unseats another. Joining a room means joining its chat.
  (`seats-and-watchers-contract`, plan 739–744.)
- **Saved rooms keep loading.** Any persisted-shape change goes through cardtable's codec (v2, w95b) with a decoder test
  on a real sanitized room file. A quarantined room is a failure.
- Nothing a player sees changes, except bomberman's rooms surviving a restart and its lobby updating live.
