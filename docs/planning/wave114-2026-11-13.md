## Why

A second game can't have rooms without borrowing Magic's: every room's stored record is shaped around Magic.
- **Magic's fields:** `format`, `mode`, `podSeats`/`podAi`/`podDrafts`, `plan`, `gauntlets`, `lockedDecks`,
  `scenario`, the Challenge fields: 96 reads in `lobby.ts`.
- **Bomberman's seats:** a bomberman room seats through Magic's pod (`usesPod`, `sitPod`).
- **Hardcoded type checks:** `lobby.ts` has 15 `gameType === 'draft' | 'constructed-1v1'` branches.

This session makes the **record and the seats** generic. Moving the lobby's logic out of Magic is the next step,
and it needs this one first.

## Settled

- **The target shape:** a platform room record holds what every game's room has:
  - id, name, creator, leader, gameType, members (with per-userId ready), password salt/hash, createdAt, chat
    refs;
  - **game seats**: who holds each seat, by seat number; a member not seated is watching;
  - an opaque `game` blob the game type owns and validates.
  Magic's fields move into Magic's `game` blob. The platform names none of them.
- **Persisted rooms decode old files, and nothing is lost.** Prod's room store (`~/srv-data/cardtable/rooms`, 16
  records at scaffold) must load after the change with every Magic field intact. Prove it:
  1. copy the records to scratch (`cp`, then `chmod 444`);
  2. decode them through the base build and the new build;
  3. compare the room each one becomes, field for field.
  **Never write to `~/srv-data`.** Also add decode-old tests on committed sanitized copies of real records (one
  constructed, one draft pod, one gauntlet or tournament, one bomberman).
- **Rollback.** A prod rollback to v0.68.0 must still load rooms that the new build wrote. Do one of two things,
  and say which, with a test:
  - write both shapes for one release (the old top-level Magic fields beside the new `game` blob), dated, to be
    dropped in a later release; or

    quarantine, not mangle. Quarantine still loses rooms on a rollback, so prefer writing both.
- **The view is unchanged on the wire**, except where w113v already moved `ai`. Readers move first; nothing
