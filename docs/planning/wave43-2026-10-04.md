## §A

1. **W6.** No element on the board ever scrolls, and the page never reflows when a stack grows: a stack
   grows by tightening its fan, never by overflowing its slot. `table-fit.spec` already asserts "no page
   scroll"; add "no element inside the board has a scroll bar" for 2, 3, 4 and 8 cards in one slot.
2. **W7.** Dropping a card onto another to stack it is the easy case: the "on top" zone takes most of the
   card, the before/after zones a thin strip at each edge. Prove with a real drag in `stacking.spec`.
3. **W12.** Every card in a stack keeps the same size, 2 to 8 cards. Measure in `stacking.spec` (each card's
   box equal within 0.5 px).
4. **W15.** A planeswalker shows its current loyalty the way a creature shows P/T, updating when it changes.
   Reuse the P/T badge's existing classes: `card.css` belongs to w44 today, so a new style there is a
   stop-and-report.
   Prove with a scenario: activate a loyalty ability, the number changes on the board.
5. **W19.** Give the ring its full room inside the bar's clip (`.seat__ident`'s padding, and the sides if the
   bar's width clips too) — measure first, in a live 1280×800 frame: the ring's painted extent (mark box +
   6 px) against the clipping ancestor's box, for both seats, top and bottom bar. Done when no side of the
   ring is clipped (0 px) and the seat's tracks did not move other content (`table-fit.spec` green).
6. **Done when:** `pnpm run check` 0; `stacking.spec` and `table-fit.spec` 3 runs in a row with the jar env;
   a 1280×800 frame of a 4-card stack viewed and described; the report quotes W6, W7, W12, W15, W19.
