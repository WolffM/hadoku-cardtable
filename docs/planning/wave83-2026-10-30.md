# Wave 83 — tournament core: clocks, the rope, leader tools, and a tournament that won't break

**Shape: five single sessions (`w83a`–`w83e`), not a manager.** Each lane owns distinct files. The one
shared contract (the room settings schema) is owned by lane A, and the order is written below.

Source: the operator's planning dump of 2026-10-29/30 (first public release with a big tournament) and
their answers the same day. Waves 84 (brackets at any size) and 85 (events, the front page, Rally) come
after this one. Wave 86 (linked accounts, Majors, medals) belongs mostly to hadoku_site and watchparty.

## Settled — do not re-open (operator, 2026-10-30)

1. **Chess clock (D2):** **20 minutes per player per match**, one pool across the games of a series.
   - It runs whenever the game is waiting on that player (priority, any decision, mulligans, choices on
     the opponent's spells), and keeps running while that player is disconnected.
   - It never runs on the server's own auto-passes.
   - Sideboarding between games has its own allowance and doesn't draw on the clock.
   - **At zero, that player loses the match.**
   - **On by default in tournaments.** Any room can switch it on in its settings.
2. **Draft rope (D3):** a per-pick timer, not a total, using Wizards' MTR Appendix B times by cards
   left in the pack:
   - 15–14: 40 s
   - 13: 35
   - 12: 30
   - 11–10: 25
   - 9–8: 20
   - 7: 15
   - 6–5: 10
   - 4–2: 5
   - 1: no timer

   The rope shows for the last third of a pick. **On timeout, the seat gets the draft bot's pick.**
   **Configurable in the room's settings** (operator: "allow this to be configurable"): at least on/off,
   plus a speed multiplier.
3. **Leader tools (D5):** "there is no concept of an admin for now. the room leader is the admin." Each
   works whether the leader is seated or watching:
   - **DQ a player:** their current match is a loss, every remaining pairing is a bye for its opponent,
     and they leave the standings with their record kept.
   - **Reset a match:** the current game restarts from scratch with the same decks, keeping the series
     score. Forge can't rewind.
4. **The room leader is the only authority.** No admin-key paths.

## §A — chess clock

- **Failing spec first:** a real-Forge e2e with a short clock (a test-only setting, e.g. 20 s). Seat 1
  idles on priority, their clock reaches zero, and they lose the match. Both screens show it. Assert on
  the clock reading: it ticks only on the seat being waited on, never during the server's auto-passes.
  Then a unit test of the clock arithmetic (pause on a hand-over, resume, disconnect keeps running,
  sideboarding is excluded).
- **The clock's facts come from the server,** sent with the match's frames. The client only draws what
  it's told; a client never decides time.
- **UI:** a compact clock beside each player's life total. It turns warning-coloured in the last minute,
  using `--warn` / `--danger` tokens (state colours are theme tokens). No hint text. Publish an artifact
  of the look and wait for the operator's approval before landing (look changes are gated).

## §B — draft rope

- **Failing spec first:** a two-human draft with the rope on. One drafter idles, the timeout takes the
  bot's pick, and the next pick's timer matches the table above. A unit test pins the table and the
  multiplier.
- **The deadline is set by the server.** Bots keep their own pace (`botPickMs`).
- **UI:** a burning rope along the pack, for the last third of each pick. Artifact first, as in §A.

## §C — leader tools

- **Failing spec first:** a 4-entrant tournament (2 humans and 2 AI is fine). The leader, watching and
  not seated, DQs a player mid-round:
  - that player's current match records as a loss;
  - their future pairings become byes;
  - the standings keep their record and mark them dropped.

  Then a reset: a game is restarted, the series score is unchanged, and both seats are back at a fresh
  game. Also cover a seated leader, and a non-leader being refused.
- **DQ and reset are destructive, so each asks for a confirmation in the page.** Every use is logged
  (`event: 'player-dq'` / `'game-reset'`, with the leader's id).

## §D — break tournament mode

Write each case as a spec (e2e where it needs real Forge, unit where it doesn't). Each one must either
pass, or fail with a report naming the cause and the owning lane:
- **Players reconnecting:** a player reloads mid-round, and another closes their tab mid-game and comes
  back 2 minutes later.
- **The leader leaving:** mid-tournament, and leadership passes on.
- **A restart:** the server restarts mid-round (the `boot.test` style, plus one real e2e), and the
  tournament resumes with the same pairings and standings.
- **Odd and full counts:** a full 8-seat pod; 5, 6 and 7 entrants; byes rotate and never repeat while
  avoidable.
- **Ties:** full ties on all tiebreak keys; standings stay stable and deterministic.
- **Dropping out:** a player drops (self-drop) versus leaves the room mid-round.
- **Losing a match:** two results posting at once at round end, and a match whose JVM dies (fails over
  or ends cleanly).
- **Odd series results:** a Bo3 that ends 1-1 by the time limit or a draw (w82's forced draw at 8 h).

Run each new spec ×3 on a claw. Report a ledger of cases: case → result → owner. Fix nothing outside
`e2e/`; file each failure to its owner.

## §E — public readiness, small

- **Rate limits:** per-user limits on `POST /api/rooms`, `POST /api/events` and the match-start route,
  with the same `MultiRateLimiter` and config style as `JOIN_RATE_LIMIT`. A test proves each limit
  answers 429.
- **Music reviews:** `sounds.ts` gates the music-review routes at friend tier and above explicitly, so
  they stay closed once the app's floor is `member`. A test proves a member gets 403.
- **Docs:** `docs/LICENSING.md` gets a dated decision entry quoting the operator: free forever, members
  only, anyone may become a member. It also amends the rows and rules that say "friend gate" (§2 table,
  the rules, §3.1, the Scryfall section), the way D15/D18/D20 did. `AGENTS.md` ("Keep the friend gate")
  and `NOTICE.md` ("Private…") change to match.
- **Not in this lane:** flipping `CARDTABLE_MIN_TIER=member`. That's a config change on hadoku_site's
  wrapper, done on PPE first, and it waits for the operator.
