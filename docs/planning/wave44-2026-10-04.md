1. **W13.** Tapping, untapping, a card arriving on or leaving the battlefield, and attackers turning all
   animate with the same feel as the float-back (about 300 ms, eased), under `prefers-reduced-motion` none.
   No layout property animates (transform/opacity only), so `table-fit` frames are unaffected.
2. **W2.** A selected card and a selectable card look different at a glance: selectable keeps the accent
   outline; selected adds a distinct fill or lift. Check against the London mulligan bottom-pick and any
   multi-card choice.
3. **Done when:** `pnpm run check` 0; `motion.spec` (real Forge) asserts the transition properties on a
   tapped land and the selected-vs-selectable difference, 3 runs in a row; frames viewed and described; the
   report quotes W13 and W2.
