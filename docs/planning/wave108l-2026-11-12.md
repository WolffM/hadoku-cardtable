## 1. A rematch in the same room

Today a room plays one match: on `over`, the relay match ends and the registry forgets it, and the next Start
does nothing (w107n's "known").
1. On a relay match's end, the room gets a fresh `RelayMatch` for its next match, with the same seats by
   `roomSeatOf`. Every live relay socket rejoins it (`relay-channel.ts` joins on every connect; decide whether a
   rejoin needs a new event or a reconnect, and say which).
2. Unit: two matches back to back in one room, with the second's messages delivered and its `over` recorded.
3. e2e: `relay-fixture.spec` (or `bomberman.spec`) plays a second match after the first ends.
4. Mutation: no fresh match on end → the second match's messages go nowhere → red. Record the counts.

## 2. The result on reload

`relay:ended` reaches only live sockets. A screen that loads the room after the end sees no result, and its join
answers `unknown-match`.
1. Keep the last ended match's outcome per room until the next match starts. A join after the end is answered
   with it, and `BundleSlot` shows "<names> won" from it.
2. Unit plus an e2e step: a third screen loads after the end and shows the result.

## 3. Only the leader ends it

`RelaySession` accepts `{ t: 'over' }` from any seat (pre-existing; w107n's "next"). The protocol says the host,
the room's leader, sends it.
1. `over` from a seat that isn't the leader's is refused and logged. A watcher's is already refused (R's guard).
2. Unit: a non-leader's `over` doesn't end the match. Mutation: drop the check → red.
3. Leadership moves only on Leave (ruling 6). Decide what a match does when the leader leaves mid-match (the
   relay's seat hold applies), and say it in the note.
