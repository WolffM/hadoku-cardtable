# Wave 82 — rooms close when idle, and no game runs forever

**Shape: one single session (`w82`).** It's one subject: the lobby's clock and one forced ending. The
pieces share `lobby.ts` and the match's end, so splitting them would put two lanes in one file.

## The operator's words (2026-10-29)

> "non-tournament rooms should close after being idle for 8hrs. do not close rooms that have a game in
> progress. but games that are in-progress for >8hrs should be forced into a draw and players sent back to
> the lobby"

Answered the same day:
- **Does the forced draw apply to tournament games too?** "Every game." A game 8 h in is abandoned,
  whatever room it is in.
- **Where do players go after a forced draw?** "Their room." That's the room the game was played in,
  with the result shown. The room's idle clock starts again from the draw.

## Settled — do not re-open

1. **A non-tournament room closes after 8 h idle.** Tournament rooms (`usesPod` in `lobby.ts`: a draft,
   or a constructed room with `settings.tournament`) keep today's rule unchanged: `ROOM_IDLE_MS` 12 h,
   and a started pod spared for `POD_SPARED_MS` 24 h. Seeded testing rooms are never swept, as now.
2. **A room with a game in progress is never closed,** tournament or not, whatever its idle age. "In
   progress" means a live match on any of its tables, the same fact the room card reads
   (`matchIsLive`, `table.matchId`). Today's sweep does not check this at all. That is a bug the new rule
   fixes for every room type.
3. **A game that has been in progress for more than 8 h ends in a draw,** in any room:
   - It ends through the game's own end path, as a draw by `GameEndReason.Draw` in Forge (the same
     "draw by the rules" `match.ts:249` already handles), never by killing the JVM or by a concede.
   - Both seats are told the game is over with the reason shown.
   - The result is recorded as a draw: in the table's series (a drawn game), and in stats if draws are
     recorded there.
   - Then both players are carried back to their room, the way a finished game already returns them.
4. **Idle means no room activity and no live game.** Room activity is a lobby mutation (`updatedAt`), as
   today. A forced draw counts as activity, so the room's 8 h idle clock starts again from it.
5. **One sweep, one clock.** Extend the existing `sweep` and its scheduler; don't add a second timer
   system. The 8 h values are named constants beside `ROOM_IDLE_MS`, and their comments quote the
   operator.
