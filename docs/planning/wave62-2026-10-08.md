## Why

Playtest 15 (ledger AE1–AE6, AE9, AE10), 2026-10-08, match `m-8f1e13e0307f`, on `73d7baa`:
- AE1 *"slight inconsistencies for where the cursor is displayed for the opponent at different aspect
  ratios and screen sizes … there is scaling already but the cards themselves don't actually get smaller
  in size until the screen is much, much smaller. it's important that the cursor is correctly showing
  what card is being hovered even if the screen size is vastly different."* (third ask: AA5, AB4.) The
  pointer crosses as a fraction of the whole board overlay (`packages/shared/src/presence.ts:22–41`;
  `apps/ui/src/play/live/presence.ts:151` `spotOf`, `:177` `placeSpot` mirrors `y`). The receiver lays
  its rows and card grids out for ITS viewport — `board.css:1011` `--card-w = min(--card-max, …)` with
  `--card-max: 7.5rem` (`:854`) — so a battlefield row's cards occupy a different fraction of the board
  on a 21:9 screen than on a 4:3 one, and the same fraction lands beside the card, not on it.
- AE6 *"i just played 'Jushi Apprentice' but there seemed to be a bit of lag when animating it from the
  opponent side. i even saw a targeter arrow briefly? thats very weird. this card has no targeter. please
  clean up that logic. I also noticed there was a bit of desync between where the phantom card was and
  where the cursor actually released the card."* (third ask for the release point: AA4, AB6.)
  `live/presence.ts:218` `handsOf` reports a PARKED card's centre as `from`; the receiver draws
  `.presence__arrow` from `from` to the pointer whenever `from` is present — so a parked creature draws an
  arrow on the other screen until the frame commits it. The placement's `at` is the POINTER at release
  (`gestures/install.ts`: `spotOf(canvas, point)` before `selectCard`) while the ghost is drawn at the
  lift's grab offset from the pointer — the back stands where the pointer was, not where the card was.
- AE10 *"as i was picking the X value for condescend i notice that from the opponent side they could see a
  targeter arrow as i was dragging the slider … the targeter arrow should only be drawn if i'm actually
  selecting a target."* Same mechanism as AE6: `paint()` (`install.ts:570`) draws no arrow on MY screen
  while an ask is open (`match.asks.length > 0` empties the offered set, `live/match.ts:1182`); the arrow
  the other screen draws is presence's, from the parked card's `from`.
- AE2 *"when hovering a card, try to make sure the expanded version doesn't cover the thing that is being
  hovered … theres an active decision on my screen, and if i hover serum visions it blocks the card itself.
  for hovering cards on the board let's try to be a bit smarter about where to place the expanded img."*
  `inspect/index.ts:397–412`: with a `.live__prompt` open the panel is re-parented beside it and placed by
  `placeBesideModal` (`:307`) — left, then right, then the roomier side, shrunk — which never reads the
  hovered card's own rect.
- AE3 *"the 'Waiting for Hadoku to decide for Serum Visions...' text at the top is a bit easy to miss. can we
  instead move text like that to the 'decision popout' area? similar to how it shows 'Waiting for
  {opponent}...' during the mulligan phase? i think that would be cleaner."* The sentence is the top line
  `live__waiting` (`LiveBoard.tsx:2127–2133`, `waitingSentence` `:457`); the mulligan wait is the pop-out
  `live-pregame-wait` (`:2417–2424`), a strict if/else with the small line (`:494–520`).
- AE9 *"i cast 'condescend' which lets me scry as part of the resolution of the spell, but also asks the
  opponent about paying the cost. lets be careful and resolve these things one at at time, i dont think it
  should be showing simultaneously."* Asks are per seat and never serialized across seats
  (`apps/server/src/play/match.ts:1444` `onFrame` → that seat's `asks`; `:2334` `openAsk`); no test opens
  an ask on both seats at once; whether Forge opened both, or one stayed drawn, is NOT measured.
- AE4 *"dragging cards for scrying is a bit inconsistent. can we us the same phantom card and placement
  logic we use for putting cards on the board? also, when cards have been selected to be put on bottom and
  we show the up/down arrow for reordering let's display those up/down arrows on top of the card rather
  than in their own row. that makes it look a bit more compact."* `prompts/ManipulateCardListPrompt.tsx`:
  native HTML5 `draggable` rows (`:202`) with their own `onDrag*`; the ↑/↓ live in a
  `prompt__row-actions` row (`:235`, `:243`); no e2e exercises the scry sheet.
- AE5 *"when drawing a box to tap lands, can we have them all tap simultaneously rather than one at a
  time?"* `install.ts:1018` `pumpTaps` sends one `selectCard` and waits for the board to show it tapped
  before the next (Forge takes one at a time); each land turns as Forge confirms it.

## Settled — do not re-open in any lane

- **The pointer is anchored to the card it is over** (2026-10-08, AE1, the operator's diagnosis: "the cards
  themselves don't actually get smaller"). Over a public card (battlefield, stack, exile, graveyard, the
  face-down opponent hand slots by index), presence carries the card's id (or hand index) and the pointer's
  fractions within that card's box; the receiver draws the cursor at the same fractions of ITS copy of the
  card. Off any card, the board fraction as today. The header rule in `packages/shared/src/presence.ts`
  ("never card-relative") is rewritten to say this; `held` stays a boolean (a lifted HAND card's id never
  crosses — hidden information).
- **An arrow is drawn on the other screen only while I am aiming** (AE6, AE10): `from` is present only when
  `match.selectables !== null` or a stack ask is open on MY screen — the same condition `paint()` uses. A
  parked card with no target sends no `from`. The placed-card arrows (`placed.ts`) are a different thing
  and stay.
- **The back stands where the ghost was** (AE6): the placement's `at` is the ghost's box centre at release
  on the sender; the receiver stands the back at that spot and glides it into the gap from there. The
  pointer's own spot is not the card's.
- **The inspector covers nothing it is about** (AE2): with a decision open, the panel avoids the pop-up
  AND the hovered card's rect — the four candidate placements (left/right of the pop-up, above/below the
  card) scored by overlap, shrunk before overlapping either. No card is ever covered by its own inspector.
- **The waiting sentence is the pop-out** (AE3): the `live-pregame-wait` shape (`section.confirm`,
  `role="status"`) carries `waitingSentence` whenever `theirWaitingOn` is present, and the top line is
  deleted — not hidden. One component, one testid (`live-waiting-pop`), both uses (pregame and mid-game).
  No explanatory text beyond the sentence.
- **One decision at a time** (AE9) is a server rule, decided from evidence: lane D first records the wire
  order for Condescend against real Forge (`w62-condescend.state`: alice casts Condescend X=1 on bob's
  spell). If Forge opens bob's pay ask and alice's scry ask at once, the server holds the later ask until
  the earlier closes (a per-match queue across seats, oldest first; the held seat gets `waitingOn`). If
  Forge asks them in sequence and the UI only LOOKED simultaneous, the fix is on the client (a closed ask's
  sheet must leave before the next is drawn) and the server stays as it is. The note says which.
- **Scry drags like the board** (AE4): a press on a row lifts it with the board's lift (`drawCastLift`'s
  `'lifted'` sizing, the same slop), the Top/Bottom targets are drop zones under the pointer, release
  commits; ↑/↓ are two small buttons drawn over the card's own tile (top-right), the actions row is
  deleted. Keyboard reorder stays (the buttons keep their `aria-label`s).
- **Boxed lands turn at once** (AE5): at release every boxed land is drawn `tapped` at once (an optimistic
  state the frame confirms); Forge is still sent one `selectCard` at a time as today; a land Forge does not
  tap (the sweep ends on a real question) is drawn untapped again when the frame says so.
- Theme tokens only; no explanatory UI text; a lane that stops drawing an attribute greps `e2e/` for it
  in the same land (the arrows W5 incident, 2026-10-08).
