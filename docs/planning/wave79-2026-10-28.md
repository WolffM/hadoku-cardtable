## Shape: four single sessions, not a manager

Each lane below is one session in its own worktree (`.claude/worktrees/w79<x>`), doing its items in order and
landing itself with `scripts/land.sh`. The four own disjoint files and run at the same time. The protocol
below is written for a manager; in these sessions **you are the manager and the only lane** — do the work
yourself (no sub-agents unless your brief says so), and keep every rule about verification, landing, claims,
the clock and the closing note. Target 3 h, hard stop 4 h.

commit (`land.sh` numbers it) with the operator's words; only the operator writes ✅. A player-visible change

**The operator's question — how the animations interact, and which caused each symptom.** Measured, it is not
the flying. The promo run's own seat-0 telemetry (`client` `beats` / `departure-layout`, play-server log of
the promo worktree, 17:51:30Z 2026-10-28) shows Forge sends the wipe as **two views** ~50 ms apart: the
resolution with all 11 hits and 5 deaths (the 3 human creatures and the two angels — the first two of the
opponent's row), then the other 6 deaths with **no hits**, whose stand-ins sat **202 px** off their cards.

- **11a** — the second view's kept places were indexed against `prev`'s battlefield, which had already lost
  the angels (`departures.ts:273` on base, re-inserted by `Board.tsx:3096`'s splice), so those six gaps came
  out two columns left — on top of the angels' stand-ins, which were drawn later in the overlay and covered
  them. The angels "instantly disappeared".
- **11b** — with no hits in their own view, `numbers.ts:1132` (base) gave those six `?? startMs` = 0: they died
  at once. Their bolts, scheduled in the first view against the row cards, found those replaced by gaps and
  were dropped (`numbers.ts:790`, `!to.isConnected → land()`), or arrived after the death.
- **11c** — 950 ms later the six were gone, uncovering the angels' stand-ins, whose bolts landed on schedule —
  "reappeared underneath the ones that just died and got hit by the fireball properly".
- The flyers' sway (`card.css` `fly-*` on the `<li>`'s children; `flight.ts` pins the copy's parts) never moves a
  stand-in. Two more causes of the same class turned up while proving it: a death's timer and its hit's
  chained timers drift apart under load (deaths 80–870 ms before their impacts), and a stand-in placed at its
  gap jumps when its row is mid-glide after an earlier death closed up (62–186 px, the opponent's screen).

**The fix, as a class** (one mechanism for every death, flyer or ground, any number of views): places are indexed
as the battlefield is drawn (`departuresOf(prev, next, stillLeaving)`, `withLeaving` moved into `departures.ts`)
and close up in turn (`closeUp`); a hit due on a card carries across views (`hitsDue`) and re-finds its target
when it fires; a death starts on its own hit's impact (`strike`), its due time only as a fallback; a stand-in is
drawn where its card was drawn; a place is held while its exit is still to come (`holds`).
