# Wave 80 — a steady glow through normal play

**Shape: three single sessions (`w80a` the steady glow, `w80b` Frontline Medic's glow, `w80c` unlock on the last 10%; the last two at the end of this file), not a manager.** One item, one mechanism, files no wave-79 lane
owns. Fewer than three independent lanes, so a wave's overhead buys nothing (the waves skill's step 2).

## The operator's words (2026-10-28, after trailer cut 1)

> "i notice there is a lot of flashing going on when proceeding through phases. this happens whenever
> cards are clickable and briefly become not-clickable between phases. it feels janky and unecessary
> and i'd like to try doing a pass at normal game flow and looking at all the places that the cards
> 'flash' and try to minimize it."

## Settled — do not re-open

- **The glow is drawing only.** What a click may SEND stays `boardInteraction` (Forge's real state). A
  held glow never makes Forge accept a click it did not offer. That is AF101's rule (`offer-hold.ts`
  header) and it stands.
- **A real change still shows.** When the decision passes to the other seat, or Forge's new set really
  drops a card, the glow goes. The goal is no off-then-on blink for a card that is offered on both
  sides of a gap. It is not to keep stale cards lit.
- **Fix the mechanism, not each phase** (system-level-fixes): one rule in `offer-hold.ts`/`match.ts`,
  not a per-step exemption list.
- **Look change → artifact first.** Before landing, publish a before/after (same scripted game, both
  recordings) and wait for the operator's approval (show-motion-as-an-artifact).

## What the tracker verified before writing this (origin/main b08dd34e0)

- The glow is `data-can-act` (`Board.tsx:2623`, `card.css:1259`), from `interaction.actionable`.
  `LiveBoard.tsx:3113` sets that to `offered.selectable` from `drawnOffer(match)` (`LiveBoard.tsx:3070`).
- `drawnOffer` (`match.ts:1683`) is `boardInteraction`. The only exception is while an `OfferHold`
  stands (`offer-hold.ts`, AF101, lane NF, 2026-10-16).
- **The hold only starts on a click on a card or player** (`selectCard`/`selectPlayer`). It ends when
  **"the turn or the phase moving on"** (`drawnOfferOf`: `facts.phase !== hold.phase`) happens, when
  Forge names any set, when an ask must be answered by hand, when the game waits on another seat, or
  after `HOLD_MAX_MS` (2 s).
- `boardInteraction` is empty whenever `inputLive` is false or Forge's sets are null. Each of these
  makes it empty:
  - `clearWeaklySelectable` at the end of every priority window (`match.ts:3386`);
  - `holdBar` on a button press (`match.ts:1334`, which sets `inputLive=false` until the next `updateButtons`);
  - `clearSelectables` (`match.ts:3320`).
- **Hypothesis, not yet measured:** with no hold, a pass (OK/Pass/End Turn, or an auto-pass the server
  makes) leaves the glow dark from Forge's clear to its next `setWeaklySelectable`. A click-started
  hold is also cut short the moment the phase moves. Both are "between phases".

## Two orange marks: find out which one it is before changing anything

| mark | where | means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| condition rim | `CardTile.css` `.card[data-condition-met]`, from the bridge's `conditionMetJson` (`PlayBridge.java:1461`, `ConditionsOn`) | "a conditional ability of this card is on" (AF97) |
| can-act glow | `card.css` `--can-act-glow` (`var(--warn)`), `data-can-act` from Forge's actionable set | "Forge says you could do something with this now" |

Medic's script (`forge-gui/res/cardsfolder/f/frontline_medic.txt`):
- `T:Mode$ Attacks | ValidCard$ Card.Self | IsPresent$ Creature.attacking+Other | PresentCompare$ GE2` (battalion);
- `A:AB$ Counter | Cost$ Sac<1/CARDNAME> | ValidTgts$ Card.hasXCost`.

What the tracker read, without running anything:
- Forge's actionable scan requires a legal target (`forge-ai/.../AvailableActions.java:252,266` `isTargetable`).
  So the sacrifice should only light with an X spell on the stack.
- The battalion presence check should be false outside combat.

**So none of the obvious readings explains it. Measure.** There are three candidates:
- (a) the rim lit by the battalion trigger's presence check, or by the Attacks trigger itself;
- (b) can-act while an X spell was on the stack;
- (c) can-act as a candidate attacker during declare attackers. In that case every creature that can
  attack glows, which is the can-act meaning working as designed.

1. **Find the operator's sighting** if you can: a PPE or prod recording (`apps/server/data/recordings/`
   in either deploy tree), or a match log with Medic. The tracker found none in prod's 40 newest.
2. **Reproduce it in real Forge**: a `w80b-medic.state` with Medic and two other creatures, in your

   It must be red on main for whatever the operator saw.
3. **Fix the class, not the card**:
   - If it's the rim (a): an event condition (a trigger's own `IsPresent`/`Attacked$`-style filter
     that's only true while the event happens) is not a "conditional effect is active". Fix it in
     `ConditionsOn`, the way AF214 and AF308 did, and prove it against every card those rows named
     (no regression).
   - If it's (b) or (c), the glow is honest and the trouble is that **two different meanings share
     one colour**. That's the operator's design call: don't change any styling. Stop and write it up
     with stills of both marks side by side in an artifact, and recommend one change (for example, a

# w80c — unlock on an animation's last 10% (single session, beside w80a and w80b)

## The operator's words (2026-10-28)

> "i'm noticing some animation waiting is a bit too long. let's "unlock" the game state when it's
> waiting on the last ~10% of an animation"

## Settled — do not re-open

- **The animation still plays to its end.** Only the WAIT on it ends early. Nothing is shortened, cut
  or faded sooner.
- **One rule at the gate, not per animation.** Every wait on the board's beats goes through
  `motion.ts`: `noteBoardDrawing`, `whenBoardStill` and `animationsSettled`. The 90% point belongs
  there, as one named constant. No per-beat timing edits.
- **The seat's own acts already skip pacing** (AF216, own-acts-skip-pacing). This is about what still
  waits: the opponent's acts, resolutions, and the frames behind them.

## What the tracker read (origin/main 6b00a07ad)

- The pacer (`apps/ui/src/play/live/pace.ts`) holds every frame of a class in `HELD_BY_THE_BOARD`
  (`cast`, `resolve`, `turn`, `draw`, `default`). It holds them until `whenBoardStill`
  (`motion.ts:214`), capped at `BOARD_HOLD_MAX_MS` 5 s. A decision queued behind those frames waits
  with them. `input-held` reports already split that wait into `boardMs` and `gapMs`: use them.
- The board is "drawing" while any promise passed to `noteBoardDrawing` is pending. `numbers.ts:1151`
  passes `beatsOver(endMs)`, which waits out the scheduled `lastMs` and then
  `animationsSettled(canvas)` (`motion.ts:267`: every finite beat's `finished`). Dice and the coin
  register too (`dice.ts`, `coin-flip.tsx`).
- `LiveBoard.tsx:2000` also waits on `whenBoardStill` for the game's result screen ("the game-loss …
  only after the fireball actually hits", 2026-10-15). Decide, from the measurement, whether the
  result screen keeps a full wait. A fireball's impact lands well before its last 10%, so either may be
  right. Say which in the note.
- The pacer's per-class gaps (`PACE_DELAYS_MS`: `resolve` 600, `phase`/`turn` 250, `draw` 200,
  `default` 150) are not animations, and this item doesn't change them. If the measurement shows the
  felt wait is mostly gaps, report that. Don't retune them.

## The work, in order

1. **Measure (a failing test first).** With Fast Mode off, run the opponent's attack with a death,
   a resolution chain (`promo-counterwar.state` on branch `promo-clips` is a ready deep stack) and a
   draw. Record for each held frame: when its last beat reached 90%, when it ended, and when the frame
   (and any decision behind it) actually went out. Use the `input-held` / `board-hold-capped` reports

   one animation frame) of the longest running beat. It must be red on main, with the numbers.
2. **Fix at the gate.** One way to do it: `noteBoardDrawing` carries each entry's expected end, so
   `whenBoardStill` can release at the moment every entry is ≥ `UNLOCK_AT` (0.9) of the way through.
   `animationsSettled` resolves when each running beat's `currentTime` reaches 0.9 of its

3. **Check the overlap.** The next frame now draws while the last 10% of the previous beat still plays.

   - no number is cut off;
   - no card is drawn twice (w79c's 11c, "the vanished creature reappeared underneath", lives exactly

   `promo-clips`: private Xvfb and a null pulse sink, never the operator's screen or headset). Publish

4. **Prove.** The new spec ×3 on a claw. Also run every timing spec that waits on beats: grep `e2e/` for

5. **Land** with `scripts/land.sh`. Include an AF row `| AF??? |` and a
