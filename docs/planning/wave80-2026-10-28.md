# Wave 80 — a steady glow through normal play

**Shape: three single sessions (`w80a` the steady glow, `w80b` Frontline Medic's glow, `w80c` unlock on the last 10%; the last two at the end of this file), not a manager.** One item, one mechanism, files no wave-79 lane
owns. Fewer than three independent lanes, so a wave's overhead buys nothing (the waves skill's step 2).

## The operator's words (2026-10-28, after trailer cut 1)

> "i notice there is a lot of flashing going on when proceeding through phases. this happens whenever
> cards are clickable and briefly become not-clickable between phases. it feels janky and unecessary
> and i'd like to try doing a pass at normal game flow and looking at all the places that the cards
> 'flash' and try to minimize it."

## Settled — do not re-open

- **The glow is drawing only.** What a click may SEND stays `boardInteraction` (Forge's real state). A
  held glow never makes Forge accept a click it did not offer. That is AF101's rule (`offer-hold.ts`
  header) and it stands.
- **A real change still shows.** When the decision passes to the other seat, or Forge's new set really
  drops a card, the glow goes. The goal is no off-then-on blink for a card that is offered on both
  sides of a gap. It is not to keep stale cards lit.
- **Fix the mechanism, not each phase** (system-level-fixes): one rule in `offer-hold.ts`/`match.ts`,
  not a per-step exemption list.
- **Look change → artifact first.** Before landing, publish a before/after (same scripted game, both
  recordings) and wait for the operator's approval (show-motion-as-an-artifact).

## What the tracker verified before writing this (origin/main b08dd34e0)

- The glow is `data-can-act` (`Board.tsx:2623`, `card.css:1259`), from `interaction.actionable`.
  `LiveBoard.tsx:3113` sets that to `offered.selectable` from `drawnOffer(match)` (`LiveBoard.tsx:3070`).
- `drawnOffer` (`match.ts:1683`) is `boardInteraction`. The only exception is while an `OfferHold`
  stands (`offer-hold.ts`, AF101, lane NF, 2026-10-16).
- **The hold only starts on a click on a card or player** (`selectCard`/`selectPlayer`). It ends when
  **"the turn or the phase moving on"** (`drawnOfferOf`: `facts.phase !== hold.phase`) happens, when
  Forge names any set, when an ask must be answered by hand, when the game waits on another seat, or
  after `HOLD_MAX_MS` (2 s).
- `boardInteraction` is empty whenever `inputLive` is false or Forge's sets are null. Each of these
  makes it empty:
  - `clearWeaklySelectable` at the end of every priority window (`match.ts:3386`);
  - `holdBar` on a button press (`match.ts:1334`, which sets `inputLive=false` until the next `updateButtons`);
  - `clearSelectables` (`match.ts:3320`).
- **Hypothesis, not yet measured:** with no hold, a pass (OK/Pass/End Turn, or an auto-pass the server
  makes) leaves the glow dark from Forge's clear to its next `setWeaklySelectable`. A click-started
  hold is also cut short the moment the phase moves. Both are "between phases".

# w80d — release a permanent anywhere (dispatched 2026-10-29 after w79a landed b70752fac)

## The operator's words (2026-10-28)

> "allow players to release permanent cards anywhere, but if they release it in an invalid location,
> just have the card land in the next avaliable board space."

## Why it waits

**Re-read after w79a landed (main 4d0663da0):** the refusal is now `install.ts:936`
(`if (!spell && !overOwnBattlefield(node)) floatCastLiftHome(…)`). `Board.tsx`'s `notePlayedTo` (doc at
~:1645) already says that "a point that hits no slot … records nothing", and then the permanent "simply joins
the end of its type row". **That is the "next available board space"**, so the likely change is small: drop
the refusal, and make sure a point over the opponent's board, the stack or the rail never snaps into a slot of
the wrong row (`snapTo`). The pass-through to `notePlayedTo` stays the one placement path. w79a's
"one arrival path" (`entries.ts`'s single stand-in) is what draws the glide, so don't add a second one.

Before w79a, `resolveLift` (`apps/ui/src/play/live/gestures/install.ts`, ~:931) sent nothing when a permanent
(no target needed) is released outside the viewer's own battlefield, and floats the card home:
`if (!spell && !overOwnBattlefield(node)) floatCastLiftHome(…)`. The placement it would otherwise send
is `notePlayedTo(source, point)`, "the slot under the release point". `install.ts`, `cast.ts` and the
arrival path are w79a's ("one arrival path", 2a–c), which is rebuilding exactly this landing right now.
Scaffold w80d off main once w79a has landed.

## The rule to build (settled)

- A permanent lifted from the hand and released **anywhere outside the hand** is played: over the
  opponent's board, over the stack, over the rail, or off the board's edge.
- A release over the viewer's own battlefield keeps today's meaning (the slot under the point).
- **Anywhere else, the card lands in the next available space of the row its type goes to.** The
  "next available space" is the row-end fallback `notePlayedTo` already has for a point that hits no
  slot, never a second placement path. (`placed.ts`'s "default spot" is the STACK's, so it's not this.) The card glides
  there from the release point, on both screens (placement rides with the act, `forge:placement`).
- **Kept:**
  - a release inside the hand is a reorder;
  - a graveyard or exile card released over its own zone goes home;
  - a card that needs a target is unchanged.
- No hint text ("no explanatory UI text").
